{{no}}How to applyFour steps

Send a CV and a photograph.

  1. 01

    Prepare your CV.

    Save it as a Word or a PDF file.

  2. 02

    Add a photograph.

    A passport-size photograph, as a JPEG file.

  3. 03

    Email your application.

    Send both files to the school's careers email address.

  4. 04

    Wait to hear from the school.

    The school contacts candidates when an opening matches their qualifications.

Where to send it

The school's careers email address is to be published by the school. Until it is, call the further queries and emergencies line, {{phone:further}}, and ask where to send your CV. All the school's lines are on Contact.
