One building for every class.

From Playgroup to Class XII, every class is taught in this one building. The classrooms are air-conditioned, and generators keep classes running through an electricity failure.

Playpen is a day school, with no boarding.

The field and the courtyards.

A grass playground and open courtyards lie at the foot of the building. Annual Sports Day is held on the field, and Pohela Boishakh fills the courtyard.

Indoor games, table tennis, chess and carrom, are played through the year. See the sports the school plays.

Classrooms and laboratories.

The school has separate laboratories for Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Computer Science. The Science Fair is one of the events in the school's year.

Students choose their O Level electives in Class VIII. Senior School lists them.

The library and the bookshop.

Library classes
Run for every level, monitored by a Librarian.
Borrowing
If a book is not returned, the Librarian tells the parents through the class teacher. Policies and documents has the rule.
The bookshop
It sits alongside the library. Parents collect the year's books and copies at the start of each session; Starting at Playpen has the details.

The hall and the activity rooms.

The multipurpose hall holds the school's gatherings, from assemblies to cultural programmes and prize-givings. The building also has a students' gallery for group work and presentation, and rooms for art, music, dance, drama and clubs.

Clubs and activities says what runs in them.

The terraces and the roof.

Brick terraces with benches and trees in planters, and the city beyond the railing. A basketball court sits on the roof.

Safety, health and water.

Security
CCTV across the campus, and security staff round the clock.
Health
A sick room for minor health problems during school hours. Serious cases are handed to the parents.
On every floor
Drinking water, separate toilets for boys and girls, and fire extinguishers.
Food
An on-site cafeteria, which the second phase is to expand.
Power and phones
Generators for classes during an electricity failure, and an internal telephone system between offices and floors.

The second phase.

The second phase of the building is in progress.† The school's own pages list further laboratories, an expanded cafeteria, an advanced library and more recreational facilities among the planned additions. No completion date has been published.
