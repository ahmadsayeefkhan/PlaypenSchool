{{no}}Every dayWhat the school already does

Seven things the school does every day.

Each one is from the school's own pages: the building, the sick room, the counsellor, the identity card, the gate, the code of conduct, and photographs.

  • On the campus

    Watched, staffed and equipped

    • CCTV across the campus
    • Security staff round the clock
    • Fire extinguishers on every floor, and an internal telephone system
    Our campus
  • Health during the day

    A sick room, and the parents decide

    A health care facility and a sick room for minor problems during school hours. Serious cases are handed to the parents, so the family decides about treatment. Drinking water and separate toilets on every floor.

  • Someone to talk to

    The Student Counsellor

    A designated member of the faculty acts as Student Counsellor, on standby for academic concerns, non-academic issues and behavioural matters that teachers raise.

    Student support
  • Identity cards

    Issued after admission, worn every day

    Every student receives an identity card after admission and wears it on the school premises. A lost or damaged card is replaced through the school office.

  • Arrival, departure and the road

    A quiet gate

    Drivers follow the school's traffic rules, avoid the horn and slow down near the premises. Students carry their own bags, which reduces crowding at the gate. The school has run its own buses since March 2014.

    Transport and safety
  • Conduct and discipline

    The code of conduct

    Physical violence or abusive language leads to immediate expulsion. A Disciplinary Committee of administrators and faculty members oversees behaviour during school hours. Mobile phones are not allowed.

    Policies
  • Photographs of children

    On this website

    A photograph of a child is used on this website only where no child can be recognised, or where the school holds the family's consent. A parent may ask for a photograph to be taken down by calling the further queries line.

    Privacy

None of these is written as a policy the school adopted; each is a thing the school says it does. Confirm each is current before relying on it.

From the school's campus, health, counsellor, identity card, transport and code-of-conduct pages, September 2026.
