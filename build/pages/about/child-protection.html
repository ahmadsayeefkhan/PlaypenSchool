{{no}}Every dayWhat the school already does

The everyday measures.

To raise a concern now, go straight to the school's telephone lines.

On the campus
  • CCTV across the campus
  • Security staff round the clock
  • Fire extinguishers on every floor
  • An internal telephone system between offices and floors

The building is described on Our campus.

Health during the school day

A health care facility on the campus handles minor health problems during school hours, and there is a dedicated sick room. Serious cases are handed over to the parents, so that the family can decide about treatment.

Drinking water and separate toilets for boys and girls are provided on every floor.

Someone to talk to

A designated member of the faculty acts as Student Counsellor and is on standby to answer any query, or to give advice and support when students need it. The counsellor helps with academic concerns, non-academic issues and behavioural matters that teachers raise. Student support has the rest.

Identity cards

Every student receives an identity card after admission and wears it every day on the school premises. A lost or damaged card is replaced through the school office.

Arrival, departure and the road

Drivers are asked to follow the school's traffic rules, to avoid the horn and to slow down near the premises. Students carry their own bags rather than leaving them in the bus luggage compartment, which reduces crowding at the gate.

The school has run its own buses since March 2014. Transport and safety has the rules for the bus and how to apply for a place.

Conduct and discipline

The code of conduct says what the school expects of its students. Physical violence or abusive language leads to immediate expulsion, and a Disciplinary Committee of administrators and faculty members oversees behaviour during school hours. Mobile phones are not allowed. The summary is on Policies and documents.

Photographs of children

The school has not yet published a policy on photographing and filming children. It is to be published by the school.

Raise a concern

The school has not published a written procedure, or a named person, for raising a concern. Until it does, these are its telephone lines. Call the one for the child's section {{hours:between}}, or the further queries and emergencies line.
