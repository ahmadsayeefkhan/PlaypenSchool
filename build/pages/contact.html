{{no}}TelephoneOne line for each section of the school

Call the line for your child's section.

The school is reached by telephone or in person. Choose the line for the section your child is in, or would join: the first three are the admissions lines, open {{hours:between}}.

The further-queries lines are the school's general numbers, and the ones to use in an emergency.
