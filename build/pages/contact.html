Ask, book, or come in.

Two forms on this website send to the admissions office and give you a code to quote when you call; the third way is to come to the Front Office.

  • Ask a question

    ENQ- code

    Enquire sends your question to the admissions office's Desk and gives you an ENQ- code. Choose your child's class and the page shows the line to call.

    Enquire

  • Book a visit

    VIS- code

    Book a visit sends your request and gives you a VIS- code. Come and see the building and the section your child would join; the office confirms the day.

    Book a visit

    Completed admission forms are handed in at the Front Office, and the Admission Department sorts them. The admissions lines are open {{hours}}.
