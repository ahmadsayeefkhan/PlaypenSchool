{{no}}The portalWhat a parent can see there

Your child's record, through the year.

The school runs a website and a management system that gives parents access to their child's data. Parents can follow it through the year, not only at report time.

Report cards are on the portal too. Examinations explains how the two semesters, and the written examination that ends each of them, work.

The portal runs on the school's own system, separate from this website. If you cannot reach it, call the line for your child's section.

Fees are paid online.

Digital fee payment became mandatory from the 2021 to 2022 academic year. Tuition is paid through the school's bank, by one of several channels. Admission payments are made online in the same way.

{{no}}Day to dayFrom the code of conduct and the school's own pages

The questions families ask.

My child will be absent. What does the school need?

The code of conduct asks for a signed application from a parent when a child is absent for three days, and for a medical certificate as well when the absence runs beyond three consecutive days. Extended illness needs written notice to the school office.

The full rules are on Policies and documents.

Our address or phone number has changed.

Tell the class teacher and the office promptly, by written application, whenever an address, a phone number, an email address or an emergency contact changes. The school uses these details to reach you.

The code of conduct also names the school email for this. This site does not print an address, so call the line for your child's section if you need it.

Can my child bring a mobile phone?

No. The code of conduct does not allow mobile phones for students up to Class XII, and bags are checked at the entry in the morning. A student who needs to reach a parent urgently can use the Admin Office on Level 2 to make a call.

Where do we buy books and sportswear?

From the school bookshop, beside the library. Parents collect the yearly books and exercise copies at the start of each session, and the shop is open from 8.30 am to 1.00 pm. Sportswear must also be bought from the school.

The uniform, which is compulsory from KG I, is in Starting at Playpen.

How do we ask for a bus place?

The school has run its own bus service since March 2014. Parents who want a place apply at the Administrative Office, which confirms routes and availability at the time of application. Transport and safety has the rest.

What happens if my child is unwell at school?

A health care facility and a sick room on the campus deal with minor health problems during the school day. Serious cases are handed over to the parents, so that the family decides on treatment.

Who do I tell if I am worried about my child's safety?

Raise it with the class teacher, with the Teacher-in-Charge for your child's classes, or with the school office. Child protection and safeguarding explains how the school looks after children and what happens when a concern is raised.

A library book has not come back.

The Librarian tells parents through the class teacher when a book has not been returned. If it still cannot be returned after a set period, the parent buys a similar copy or pays for it.
