{{no}}The associationWho it is for, and what the association asks

The Playpen Alumni Association.

The association collects the details of former students, with a recent photograph, and says it wishes to connect with students who have excelled in their fields, wherever that has taken them. How to send them is below.

Register

The association asks for your name, your class and the year you left, how to reach you, and a recent photograph. The next sheet says how to send them today.

{{no}}RegisterHow to send your details today, and what to have to hand

How to register today.

This website has no form for former students yet, because the association has not said where the details should go. Until it does, the school's general line is the way in.

Have to hand

  • Your full name
  • Your class and the year you left
  • An email address, and a phone number if you like
  • A recent photograph: the association asks for one

Send your details

By telephone

{{phone:further class="contact-card__tel"}}

The school has not published an alumni contact. Its general line, for further queries, is the one number open to a former student today: call it and say that you are a former student. The line has no published hours.

When the association gives its details a place to go, a form for former students will sit here, and the Privacy page will say what it keeps.

{{no}}The school across the yearsDates to place your year, from the History page

Dates to place your year.

A few dates from the school's own record; the full story is on the History page.

On this page

  • Find your graduation year
  • How to register today
  1. 1998

    Primary classes

    By 1998 the school has added primary classes. Secondary classes follow; the school's own history gives no year for them.

  2. 2003

    The first O Level candidate

    The school's first Ordinary Level candidate sits the examination and earns straight A grades in all seven subjects.

  3. 2013

    A Level classes begin

    Chairman Mr A. Mannan Khan introduces A Level. The school now teaches from Playgroup to A Level.

  4. March 2014

    The school's own buses

    The school begins to run its own bus service.

  5. 2015

    The first A Level batch

    The first full batch of students enrols for the two-year course. A Level teaching had begun in 2013.

  6. 19 July 2018

    The move to Bashundhara

    Under Principal Mrs Sorabon Tohura, the school moves into its own building of ten storeys in Bashundhara Residential Area. The architect is Mr Ehsan Khan.
