{{no}}Playpen AlumniWho it is for, and how to join

Playpen Alumni.

Playpen Alumni is the school's app for its former students aged 18 or older. The school wants to stay in touch with former students, wherever their work or studies have taken them. You do not need a photograph to ask to join. How to ask is below.

Register

To join, you send your name, the class and year you left, and an email address. No photograph is needed.

{{no}}RegisterHow to ask to join, and what you need

Ask to join Playpen Alumni.

Former students aged 18 or older can ask to join Playpen Alumni, the school's app for its former students. The school office checks each request against its records before anyone joins.

What you need

  • Your full name, and your name at school if it was different
  • The class you were in and the year you left
  • An email address, and a mobile number if you like
  • No photograph is needed now. You can add one in the app later, if you choose.

Send your details here. The school office checks them against its records. If your request is accepted, the office contacts you by email or phone and gives you a joining code for Playpen Alumni, the school’s app for former students.

For example, a family name you no longer use.

If you are not sure, choose the closest year.

Class X is the O Level year. Class XII is the A Level year.

With the country code if you live outside Bangladesh.

Study, work, or anything you would like the school to know.

Please do not write about current pupils or your own children.

Before you send

Questions about joining

By telephone

{{phone:further class="contact-card__tel"}}

Call the school's general line and say that you are a former student. The office takes your details and checks them against its records in the same way. The line has no published hours.

Every request to join goes through the form on this page. If you cannot use it, call the school's general line and the office can fill it in with you. The line has no published hours.

Already have a joining code? Join or sign in to Playpen Alumni

{{no}}The school across the yearsDates to place your year, from the History page

Dates to place your year.

A few dates from the school's own record; the full story is on the History page.

On this page

  • Find your graduation year
  • How to join
  1. 1998

    Primary classes

    By 1998 the school has added primary classes. Secondary classes follow; the school's own history gives no year for them.

  2. 2003

    The first O Level candidate

    The school's first Ordinary Level candidate sits the examination and earns straight A grades in all seven subjects.

  3. 2013

    A Level classes begin

    Chairman Mr A. Mannan Khan introduces A Level. The school now teaches from Playgroup to A Level.

  4. March 2014

    The school's own buses

    The school begins to run its own bus service.

  5. 2015

    The first A Level batch

    The first full batch of students enrols for the two-year course. A Level teaching had begun in 2013.

  6. 19 July 2018

    The move to Bashundhara

    Under Principal Mrs Sorabon Tohura, the school moves into its own building of ten storeys in Bashundhara Residential Area. The architect is Mr Ehsan Khan.

Stories from former students

To be published by the school

Be the first story.
