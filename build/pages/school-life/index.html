{{no}}The yearWhere the big days happen

The courtyard, the field and the hall.

Playpen's year is marked by a few large days, and most of them happen in the same three places.

Annual Sports Day fills the grass field. In January 2026 Classes I to XII came out for the march past and the displays, with the building behind them. Pohela Boishakh, the Bengali new year, fills the courtyard: in April 2025 an alpona was painted on the floor, there were stalls with clay pots and crafts, and the school came in red and white. The multipurpose hall holds the Science and Technology Fair, the Cultural Programme and the graduation.

Between those days there are games and clubs, workshops for the senior students and the national days the school marks. Read about sport, clubs and activities and culture and service.

{{no}}The calendarFixtures of the school year

What the school marks each year.

Events the school lists in its yearly programme
EventWhat happens
Annual Sports DayClasses I to XII on the field for the march past and the displays, with inter-house and inter-class competition. Sports
Pohela BoishakhThe Bengali new year, celebrated across the school: an alpona on the courtyard floor, stalls, dance. Pohela Boishakh
Annual Cultural ProgrammeThe school's largest performance event, staged in the multipurpose hall. The Cultural Programme
Science and Technology FairStudent projects, experiments and models, shown at stalls in the hall. Science Fair and workshops
International Mother Language DayMarked every 21 February.
Independence DayThe national day, marked with a school function.
Victory DayCommemorated every 16 December.
Graduation ceremonyFor the outgoing senior class, held in the multipurpose hall.
Other fixturesThe Annual Milad, educational tours and year-group class parties.

Dates for the coming year come from the school. It announces them as notices and publishes them on the portal with the academic calendar.

Houses

The school has houses that compete on Sports Day and in tournaments such as inter-house basketball. Two are named in the school's published results: House Earth and House Jupiter. The full list of houses and their colours has not been published. Inter-house results
