{{no}}Annual Sports DayThe one day the whole school is on the field

The march past, the displays, the races.

Held on the school field. In January 2026 Classes I to XII came out for the march past and the displays, with the building behind them; Playgroup to KG II had their own events, in an album of their own.

The day carries inter-house and inter-class competition, from the youngest classes to Class XII.

Two albums from January 2026 show the day: Playgroup to KG II and Classes I to XII.

The date of the next Annual Sports Day is announced as a notice; see Ask the school, below.

{{no}}Against other schoolsThe tournament the school hosts, and three dated results

Football, home and away.

The school takes part in inter-school tournaments and runs inter-class ones of its own. Its own Playpen Inter-School Football Tournament, PIFT 2024, was played on the school ground with teams from other schools.

In August 2023 the school's under-17 team were runners-up at the Hurdco Inter School Football tournament. In January 2024, at the Scholastica Inter School Football tournament for under-15s, its players took the awards named Best Player and Best Defender.
