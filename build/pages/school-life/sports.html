{{no}}The gamesWhat the school plays

Seven games, played indoors and out.

Annual Sports is part of the school's yearly programme outside the timetable, and the campus has both indoor and outdoor places to play. The school keeps teams for boys and for girls in every format. It takes part in inter-school tournaments and runs inter-class ones of its own.

Games classes have their own kit: sportswear is bought from the school, and students wear it on the days they have Games. Starting at Playpen has the uniform details.

{{no}}Annual Sports DayThe day the whole school is on the field

The march past, the displays, the races.

Annual Sports Day is held on the school field. In January 2026 Classes I to XII came out for the march past and the displays, with the building behind them. The youngest children, from Playgroup to KG II, take part too, in yellow shirts and blue headbands.

The day carries inter-house and inter-class competition.

The date of the next Annual Sports Day and the tournament fixtures are not listed on this site. They are announced as notices.
