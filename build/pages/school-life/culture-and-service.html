The Cultural Programme.

The Annual Cultural Programme is the school's largest performance event. It is staged in the multipurpose hall, and the year's music, dance and drama build towards it.

The programme sits beside the school's other activities: Music, Dance and Drama run as regular activities, and a group dance team from the school took first position at the 13th Bangla Olympiad in February 2024.

The date of the next programme is announced as a notice.

Pohela Boishakh.

Pohela Boishakh is the Bengali new year, celebrated across the school and centred on the courtyard.

In April 2025 an alpona, a pattern painted on the ground, covered the courtyard floor. There were stalls with clay pots and crafts, dance for the new year, and the school came in red and white. A paper tiger mask and a red elephant, mounted on a board, were on show.

Community service.

Community service is an ongoing practice at Playpen, and one of the school's five annual events. Every year students are directly involved in serving their communities in one form or another.

The school lists the kinds of service its students take part in:

  • Helping the poor and the needy
  • Donating clothes during winter
  • Volunteering with social organisations
  • Designing and building small homes for low-income families
Eco-Schools, 2023

On 14 October 2023 three Class IX students were first runners-up for Best Presentation at an Eco-Schools programme session.
