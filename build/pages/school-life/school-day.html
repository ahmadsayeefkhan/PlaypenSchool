{{no}}From the gate to the gateFour parts of the day

A day in four parts.

  1. 01

    Arrive and go in.

    Students wear their identity card on school premises every day and leave mobile phones at home. Drivers are asked to follow the school's traffic rules and to slow down near the premises.

  2. 02

    Learn.

    Lessons run in air-conditioned classrooms, and the school says its classes use multimedia projectors. Library classes are held for every level. On the days a class has Games, students wear the games uniform.

  3. 03

    Eat, borrow, ask for help.

    The campus has a cafeteria and a bookshop. A sick room looks after minor health problems, and a student counsellor is on standby.

  4. 04

    Go home.

    There is no boarding: every child goes home at the end of the school day. The bus and gate rules are on Transport and safety.

{{no}}The yearTwo semesters

Two semesters, two examinations.

The school year is divided into two semesters, and each ends with a formal written examination. Students in the Junior and Senior sections need a set passing mark in the core subjects to be considered for promotion. Class VIII students choose their O Level electives at the end of the second semester.

Class timings for each section have not been published on this site. Please call the line for your child's section and ask for the timings.

Examinations explains how assessment works.

{{no}}Things to knowThe practical details

The practical things, one by one.

In school

How does the library work?

Library classes are held for every level and watched over by a Librarian. Children are encouraged to read the books, magazines and newspapers in the library, to improve both their English and their ideas.

If a borrowed book is not returned, the Librarian tells parents through the class teacher. A book that cannot be returned must be replaced with a similar copy or paid for.

Where do we buy books and sportswear?

From the school bookshop, which sits beside the library and is open from 8.30 am to 1.00 pm. Parents collect the yearly books and exercise copies at the beginning of every session. Sportswear is also bought from the school. Starting at Playpen has the uniform details.

What about meals and the cafeteria?

The campus has a cafeteria. What it serves, what it costs and how children pay have not been published on this site. Please ask on the line for your child's section.

Health, phones and absence

What happens if my child is unwell at school?

The health centre looks after minor health problems during the school day. For a serious case the school hands the child over to the parents, who decide on treatment. A student counsellor, a designated member of the faculty, is on standby for other concerns.

Please keep your contact details current: tell the teachers and the school office, in writing, whenever a phone number or an emergency contact changes.

Can my child bring a mobile phone?

No. The school's code of conduct does not allow mobile phones for students up to Class XII. A student who needs to reach a parent urgently can do so through the school office. Policies and documents has the code of conduct.

What do I do if my child will be absent?

Tell the school in writing. An absence of three days needs an application signed by a parent. Beyond three consecutive days the school asks for a medical certificate as well.

How do I follow my child's day from home?

Through the parent and student portal, where the school publishes academic performance, class tests, homework, report cards, attendance, punctuality and the academic calendar.

At the gate

What are the traffic rules at the gate?

Drivers are asked to follow the school's traffic rules, to avoid using the horn and to slow down near the premises. Students are asked to carry their own bags rather than use the bus luggage compartment, which reduces congestion and lateness at the gate. Transport and safety has the bus rules.

Does my child wear a uniform?

There is no uniform for Playgroup and Nursery. A uniform is compulsory from KG I. Students wear the games uniform on the days they have Games. Starting at Playpen has the details.
