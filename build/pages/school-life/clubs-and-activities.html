{{no}}On stageMusic, dance and drama

Music, dance and drama.

Music, both instrumental and vocal, Dance and Drama run as regular activities. The building has rooms set aside for art, music, dance, drama and club activity, and Drama is staged in the multipurpose hall.

The hall is also where the Annual Cultural Programme takes place, the school's largest performance event. Read about the Cultural Programme.

On 24 February 2024 the school's group dance team took first position at the 13th Bangla Olympiad.

{{no}}In the studioArt and Craft

Art and Craft.

Art and Craft is on the school's list of activities. The photograph at the top of this page shows clay models of brick forts and houses laid out on the courtyard's chequerboard floor; this one shows models of gardens and houses set out on the brick steps of a terrace.

Students who want to take art further can choose Art & Design as one of their O Level electives. Senior School lists the choices.

{{no}}Science and workshopsFairs, visits and seminars

The Science Fair and workshops for seniors.

The school holds science fairs and enters students in fairs outside it. The Science and Technology Fair is one of its five annual events: students run stalls of projects, experiments and models in the multipurpose hall.

Workshops and seminars run through the year, mainly for senior students. The school lists these topics:

  • Internet safety and responsible social media
  • University applications to international institutions
  • Model United Nations
  • United World College programmes
  • The Duke of Edinburgh's Award

In December 2024 NASA astronaut Joseph M. Acaba visited the school and spoke to students in the multipurpose hall. A student painted his portrait.

The Duke of Edinburgh's Award, and how to join a club.

The school lists Duke of Edinburgh's Award programmes among its activities, and holds workshops on the award for senior students. The levels it offers and who leads it have not been published on this site.

The same is true of the clubs: the days they meet, the ages they take and who runs them have not been published on this site. Call the line for your child's section and ask what is running this term.
