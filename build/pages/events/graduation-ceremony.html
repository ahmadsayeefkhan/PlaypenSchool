{{no}}What happensThe day, in short

Caps, gowns and maroon sashes.

The Graduation Ceremony of Ordinary and Advanced Level marks the end of the O Level and A Level years. It is held in the multipurpose hall.

The graduates wear black caps and gowns with maroon sashes, and face a red stage with the ceremony banner. Families come to watch.

Former students stay in touch with the school through the Playpen Alumni Association.
