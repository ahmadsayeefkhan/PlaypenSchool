{{no}}What happensThe day, in short

Projects, experiments and models, at stalls.

The Science and Technology Fair is one of the school's yearly events. Students build projects, experiments and models, and show them at stalls in the multipurpose hall, many of them in laboratory coats.

Judges move between the stalls. The school also enters students in science fairs outside the school.

The fair sits beside the school's laboratories for Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Computer Science, and its Science Club.
