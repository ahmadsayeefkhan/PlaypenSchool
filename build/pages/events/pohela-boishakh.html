{{no}}What happensThe day, in short

Alpona, folk art and dance in the courtyard.

Pohela Boishakh is the first day of the Bengali year, a festival for everyone in Bangladesh. At Playpen it is celebrated across the school, and the courtyard is at the centre of it.

The courtyard floor is painted with an alpona, the traditional patterns of the day. There are stalls with clay pots and folk art, painted boards, and dance.

In April 2025 the school celebrated Pohela Boishakh 1432.
