{{no}}What happensThe day, in short

The march past, the displays, the races.

Annual Sports Day is held on the school field at the foot of the building. It is one of the days in the year when the whole school is outside together.

Classes I to XII come out for the march past and the displays, in red shirts and blue trousers. The youngest children, from Playgroup to KG II, take part too, in yellow shirts and blue headbands. Senior students run athletics on the track.

The day carries inter-house and inter-class competition. Two of the school's houses are named in its published results: House Earth and House Jupiter.
