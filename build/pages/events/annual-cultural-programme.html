{{no}}What happensThe day, in short

Music, dance and drama, on one stage.

The Annual Cultural Programme is the school's largest performance event. It is staged in the multipurpose hall, which the school also uses for its other annual events.

Music, both instrumental and vocal, Dance and Drama run as regular activities through the year, and they build towards the programme.

In 2024 the programme had dance on stage, and young children in traditional dress.
