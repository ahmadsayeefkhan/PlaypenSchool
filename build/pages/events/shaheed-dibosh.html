{{no}}What happensThe day, in short

A day for the mother language.

On 21 February 1952, students in Dhaka were killed while calling for Bengali to be a state language. Bangladesh remembers them on Shaheed Dibosh, Martyrs' Day.

Since 2000 the world has marked the same day as International Mother Language Day, declared by UNESCO.

Playpen marks Shaheed Dibosh every year. Bangla is part of school life from the first classes: children are encouraged to read, write and recite from the beginning.
