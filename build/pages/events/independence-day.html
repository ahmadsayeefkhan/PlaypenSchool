{{no}}What happensThe day, in short

The national day, marked with a function.

Independence Day, on 26 March, is the national day of Bangladesh. It marks the declaration of independence in 1971, at the start of the Liberation War.

Playpen marks Independence Day with a school function each year. Its gallery holds an album of the art competition held for the day.
