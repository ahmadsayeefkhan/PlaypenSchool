{{no}}What happensThe day, in short

The victory of 1971, remembered every year.

Victory Day, on 16 December, marks the end of the Liberation War in 1971 and the victory of Bangladesh.

Playpen commemorates Victory Day every year, as one of the celebrations in its yearly programme.
