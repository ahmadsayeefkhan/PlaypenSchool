Entry points and tests

Which classes are open for admission?

Applications for the current year are accepted if there is available space. Space changes by class and by year, so please call the admissions line for your child's section before you apply: {{phone:pg-1}} for Playgroup to Class I, {{phone:ii-vi}} for Classes II to VI, or {{phone:vii-xii}} for Classes VII to XII, {{hours:between}}.

The school's current notice says that admission to A Level for the academic year July 2026 to June 2027 is open.

Is there an entrance test?

It depends on the class. Playgroup and Nursery children sit an assessment. From KG I to Class IX the child sits a written test in English, Bengali and Mathematics. The Principal and the parents then meet for an interview.

Admission to Class XI is based on the school's mock examinations and the O Level result.

Where do I get the admission form?

From the Front Office, or from the school's website. There is one form for Playgroup to Class IX and a separate one for A Level. {{ext href="https://playpen.edu.bd/admission-procedure/" label="Download the form"}}

How will I hear the result?

By telephone. Please make sure the number on the form is one you can be reached on.

Can I visit before I apply?

Yes. Call the admissions line for your child's section {{hours:between}}, and come to see the building and the section your child would join. Plan a visit

Documents

What do I bring with the form?
  • The completed admission form
  • The child's birth certificate
  • The immunisation record
  • Report cards for the last two years

Read the form in full before you submit it. The steps from the first call to the result are on How to apply.

Fees and payment

What are the fees?

The school explains fees and monthly tuition after a child has been selected. The amounts are not published on this website, so please ask on the admissions line for your child's section. Fees and payment

How are fees paid?

Online, through the school's bank. Online payment has been required since the 2021 to 2022 session. Among the channels the school lists are EBL (Visa and Mastercard), DBBL, Nagad, bKash, Rocket, Upay and Epay.

Uniform and transport

Is there a uniform?

There is no uniform for Playgroup and Nursery. From KG I a uniform is compulsory. Sportswear is bought from the school. Starting at Playpen

Is school transport available?

Yes. The school has run its own bus service since March 2014. Parents who want a place apply at the Administrative Office. Transport and safety

Cambridge and the senior years

Which Cambridge qualifications does the school teach?

Cambridge O Level, AS Level and A Level. Students choose their electives in Class VIII and carry them to O Level. A Level classes have been taught at Playpen since 2013. Read more on Senior School and AS and A Level.

How many subjects does a student take?

At least six at O Level, with English Language, Bengali and Mathematics among them, and at least three at AS and A Level.

How does admission to Class XI work?

It is based on the school's mock examinations and the O Level result. A Level admission has its own form.

Will O Level and A Level results be accepted by universities in Bangladesh?

The school has not published guidance on this yet. Please ask the line for Classes VII to XII, {{phone:vii-xii}}, and check the entry requirements of the university you have in mind.

Safety and conduct

How does the school keep children safe?

The campus has CCTV and security staff round the clock, and a sick room for minor health problems during school hours. The school's approach to child protection is set out on Child protection and safeguarding.

Can my child bring a mobile phone?

The school's code of conduct does not allow mobile phones for students up to Class XII. The code of conduct and the school's other policies are on Policies and documents.
