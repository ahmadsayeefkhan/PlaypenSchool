{{no}}UniformBy class, and where to have it made

What children wear.

What children wear, by class or item
Class or itemWhat is worn
Playgroup and NurseryNo uniform. Children wear casual clothes.
KG I onwardsThe school uniform and sportswear, which are compulsory.
Class IV onwardsGirls wear salwar-kameez. Boys wear full pants.
FootwearThe school asks all students, especially Classes X to XII, to wear white keds or sneakers with white socks.
SweatersRed and black sweaters are available at the school in the winter season. Classes IX to XII may wear black sweaters, but not hoodies or jackets.
SportswearBought from the school, and worn on the days a class has Games.

Where to have the uniform made

From KG I, uniforms are made to the school's specifications at its tailoring house, which has two branches.

  • Gulshan branch

    Pladium Market, Shop 19, Gulshan-2

  • Bashundhara branch

    Rupayan Shopping Square, Shop 310, Bashundhara

For a branch's telephone number, ask the line for your child's section.

{{no}}Books and the bookshopThe school's own shop

Books, copies and sportswear come from the school.

The school has its own bookshop. Parents collect the yearly books and exercise copies there at the beginning of each session. The bookshop is open from 8.30 am until 1.00 pm.

Sportswear is bought from the school as well. There is a cafeteria on the campus.

{{no}}The practical thingsBus, card, portal, fees, rules

Six things to know.

The school bus
The school has run its own bus service since March 2014. Parents who want a place apply at the Administrative Office. Transport and safety has the details.
The identity card
Every student receives an identity card after admission and wears it every day on school premises. A lost or damaged card must be replaced.
The portal
Parents and students use the portal for academic performance, class tests, homework, report cards and attendance. {{ext:portal}}
Paying fees
Fees are paid online. Fees and payment explains how. {{ext:pay}}
School rules
The code of conduct covers mobile phones, which are not allowed for students up to Class XII, as well as absence and contact details. Read it on Policies and documents.
The school year
The year has two semesters, each ending with a written examination. Dates are published through the school's notices and the portal. See Events and term dates, Notices and The school day.
{{no}}Before the first dayA list you can print

Six things to have done.

The Playpen School, Dhaka: before the first day.

  • Uniform made at the school's tailoring house, in Gulshan or Bashundhara (from KG I)
  • Sportswear bought from the school
  • The yearly books and exercise copies collected from the bookshop
  • A place on the school bus applied for at the Administrative Office, if you need one
  • Portal login and online fee payment set up
  • Contact details given to the school in writing, and updated whenever they change
