{{no}}AchievementAwards and firsts

Honoured every year.

Every year, Playpen students receive The Daily Star Awards: for six or more A grades at O Level, and for three or more A grades at A Level. The record goes back to 2003, when the school's first O Level candidate earned straight A grades in all seven subjects. Many former students have since gone on to study at universities abroad.

  • 6+6 or more A grades at O Level for a Daily Star Award
  • 3+3 or more A grades at A Level for a Daily Star Award
  • 7/77 out of 7 subjects at grade A for the first O Level candidate, 2003
