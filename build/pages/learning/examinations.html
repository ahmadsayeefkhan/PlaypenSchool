{{no}}The school yearTwo semesters

Each semester ends with a written examination.

The Playpen school year is divided into two semesters. A formal written examination is taken at the end of each. Progress Reports are issued across the two semesters, and in the junior classes the school uses a range of assessment tools alongside the formal papers.

Exact dates change each year. The school publishes class-wise dates on its portal and announces them as notices. Events carries the calendar.

Promotion

To be considered for promotion to the next class, students must reach set passing marks in the core subjects. A yearly aggregate below the minimum may mean that a student is not promoted. The school states this rule for its Junior and Senior sections.

  1. 01

    Teach and track.

    Semester one: teaching, class tests, class work markings and homework, all followed through the portal.

  2. 02

    Sit the first examination.

    A formal written examination ends the first semester.

  3. 03

    Continue the year.

    Semester two: teaching continues. Class VIII students choose their O Level electives at the end of it.

  4. 04

    Sit the second examination.

    A formal written examination ends the school year, and the promotion rule applies.

{{no}}Cambridge examinationsO Level, AS and A Level

Senior students also sit Cambridge papers.

In the Senior stage, students prepare for the examinations of Cambridge Assessment International Education (CAIE) at O Level, AS Level and A Level. Admission to Class XI is based on the school's mock examinations and the O Level result.

The Cambridge pathway, explained sets out how the three levels fit together.

Not published by the school

The school has not published the Cambridge examination series it enters, how students register or the deadlines. For these, call the line for Classes VII to XII, {{phone:vii-xii}}, {{hours:between}}.
