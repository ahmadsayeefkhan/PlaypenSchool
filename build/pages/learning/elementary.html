{{no}}What children learn hereThe first years

The first classrooms.

Three things the school says about its youngest classes, in the order a parent meets them: the first two years, the KG years, and the shape of the day.

Playgroup and Nursery
The focus is on communication and social skills, early thinking and logic, and fine motor skills built through structured activity. The school says children build the self-esteem and the habits that shape how they take to study later.
KG I and KG II
Activity-based learning with trained teachers. The school says safety and confidence come first, alongside emotional, physical and mental growth. The first written tests are sat from KG I.
Through the day
Teachers stay with the children through the day, so social skills grow alongside academic ones.

Built across the first years

  • Functional English

{{no}}How to joinThree steps for Playgroup to KG II

Call, meet, hear.

  1. 01

    Call or visit.

    Call the line for Playgroup to Class I, listed above, or come and see the building. The school accepts applications for the current year if there is space, and space changes by class and by year, so call first.

  2. 02

    Assessment or test.

    Playgroup and Nursery children sit an assessment. From KG I, the child sits a written test in English, Bengali and Mathematics.

  3. 03

    Interview and result.

    The Principal and the parents meet for an interview. The result comes by telephone.

{{no}}QuestionsFrom families joining Playgroup to KG II

Questions parents ask.

How is my child assessed for admission?

Children applying for Playgroup and Nursery sit an assessment. From KG I the child sits a written test in English, Bengali and Mathematics. The Principal and the parents then meet for an interview. The full list of steps is on How to apply.

Does my child need a uniform?

Not in Playgroup or Nursery: children wear casual clothes. The uniform is compulsory from KG I. Starting at Playpen has the details.

How do I talk to my child's teacher?

The school holds parent-teacher meetings, and class-wise timings are issued through the portal. For anything in between, call the line for Playgroup to Class I, listed above, {{hours:between}}.
