{{no}}What children learn hereThe Senior curriculum

Electives chosen in Class VIII, carried to O Level.

The Senior stage prepares students for Cambridge (CAIE) examinations at Ordinary, Advanced Subsidiary and Advanced Level. The school says these qualifications give students a wide range of options in their education and their careers.

In Class VIII, after the second-semester examinations, students choose their electives and take them forward to O Level. The school says its aim in the Senior stage is to balance knowledge, understanding and skills. The Cambridge pathway, explained sets out how the three levels fit together.

{{no}}QuestionsFrom families in Classes VIII to XII

Questions parents ask.

When does my child choose electives?

In Class VIII, at the end of the second-semester examinations. The school sets a minimum of six subjects at O Level, three of which are compulsory.

How many subjects does a student take?

At least six at O Level: English Language, Bengali and Mathematics (Syllabus D), plus electives. At A Level the minimum is three.

Is every listed subject taught every year?

The school confirms the subjects it offers each academic year. If a particular subject matters to your child's plans, call {{phone:vii-xii}}, {{hours:between}}, before you apply.

Which Cambridge examination series does the school enter, and how do students register?

The school has not published the examination series it enters or the registration steps and deadlines. Please call {{phone:vii-xii}}, {{hours:between}}.
