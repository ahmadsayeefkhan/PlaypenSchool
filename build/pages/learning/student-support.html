{{no}}Three servicesCounselling, guidance, health

Who a student can turn to.

  • Student counsellor

    A designated member of the faculty is always on standby to answer any query and to give advice and support when a student needs it. The counsellor helps with academic concerns, non-academic issues and behavioural matters that teachers flag.

  • Career and university counselling

    The Senior Vice-Principal and the Teacher-in-Charge give guidance on career choices, university applications, procedures and the guidelines students need for higher study at home and abroad.

  • The health centre

    A health care facility on the campus handles minor health issues during the school day. Serious cases are handed over to the parents, so that the family can decide on treatment.

{{no}}University applicationsFor students in the senior years

Papers for applying to college and university.

Senior students receive support materials for their college and university applications: recommendation letters, transcripts and testimonials, with weight on academic results and on activities outside the classroom.

The school says it runs workshops and seminars through the year. The topics it lists are below. AS and A Level and Senior School say where this guidance fits in a student's years.

Workshop and seminar topics

  • Internet safety and responsible use of social media
  • University application support for international institutions
  • Model United Nations
  • United World College programmes
  • The Duke of Edinburgh's Award
{{no}}Health and safety on the campusDuring the school day

Looking after health during the school day.

The school looks after the health of its students with a health care facility on the campus and a sick room. The building has drinking water and fire extinguishers on every floor, and separate toilets for boys and girls on every floor.

When a case is serious, the school hands it over to the parents so that the family can decide on treatment. That is why the school asks families to keep their contact details current.

Keep contact details current

Parents must tell teachers and office staff promptly, in writing, when an address, phone number, email address or emergency contact changes.
