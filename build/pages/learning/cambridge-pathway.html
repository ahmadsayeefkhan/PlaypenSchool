One curriculum, three Cambridge exams.

Playpen follows the Cambridge curriculum from its Elementary stage up to the Senior stage. In the Senior stage the school prepares students for the examinations of Cambridge Assessment International Education (CAIE) at three levels: Ordinary Level (O Level), Advanced Subsidiary (AS) and Advanced Level (A Level).

    O Level · AS · A Level

O Level
Electives are chosen in Class VIII, after the second-semester examinations. A student sits at least six subjects, three of them compulsory.
AS and A Level
Entry in Class XI, on the school's mock examinations and the O Level result. A student takes at least three subjects.
Where
Since 2020 the school has been a Cambridge exam venue, approved by the British Council. Students sit their exams in the school's own hall.

What the grades mean.

O Level and A Level
Grades run from A* (the highest) down to E. U means ungraded.
AS Level
Grades are written in lower case, from a (the highest) down to e.
"Others"
In the school's results summary, "Others" means all grades below C (below c at AS Level).

The school's results for May–June 2026 are on the Results page.

The grade scale is Cambridge International's published grading; the meaning of "Others" is from the school's results summary.

Bengali is compulsory at O Level.

English is the language of instruction at Playpen. Bengali is one of the three compulsory O Level subjects, with English Language and Mathematics, and the school says it puts weight on preserving the Bengali language and on awareness of Bangladesh's cultural heritage.

Questions parents ask.

Families who are new to Cambridge ask the same few questions. Each answer gives what the school says and, where it says nothing yet, the line to call.

Is it O Level or IGCSE?

The school's pages speak of O Level and list O Level subjects, including Mathematics (Syllabus D). For the syllabus of one particular subject, call the line for Classes VII to XII.

When and where are the exams sat?

In the school's own hall, which has been a Cambridge exam venue approved by the British Council since 2020. For the dates of each series and the way to register, call the line for Classes VII to XII. Examinations and reporting has the school's own examinations.

Will the results be accepted by universities in Bangladesh?

The school's career counselling covers university applications at home and abroad, and senior students receive recommendation letters, transcripts and testimonials. For the equivalence procedure itself, ask the school. See Student support.

What does it cost beyond tuition?

The school explains fees and monthly tuition to a family after the child has been selected, and exam registration is arranged through the school. Fees and payment says how fees are explained and paid.

How did the school do?

In the May–June 2026 Cambridge exams, 67 in every 100 O Level grades were A* or A, 46 in every 100 AS Level grades were an a, and 42 in every 100 A Level grades were A* or A. Results and achievements has the full picture and its source.

Words you will hear.

CAIE
Cambridge Assessment International Education, the body whose examinations Playpen's senior students prepare for.
O Level
Ordinary Level. At Playpen, students take at least six subjects, three of them compulsory.
AS Level
Advanced Subsidiary. At Playpen it follows O Level, from Class XI.
A Level
Advanced Level. Taught at Playpen since 2013.
Syllabus D
The name the school gives the O Level Mathematics syllabus it teaches.
Elective
A subject a student chooses, as against the three compulsory ones. Playpen students choose in Class VIII.
Mock examination
The school's own trial examination. Its results, with the O Level result, decide admission to Class XI.
Semester
One of the two parts of the school year. A written examination ends each.
