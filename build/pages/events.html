{{no}}How the year runsTwo semesters

Two semesters, two examinations.

How the school year is structured, for all classes
PeriodWhat happens
Semester 1Teaching, class tests, class work marking and homework, all followed on the portal.
End of Semester 1A formal written examination. Progress reports are issued afterwards.
Semester 2Teaching continues. Class VIII students choose their O Level elective subjects at the end of this semester.
End of Semester 2A formal written examination. Promotion decisions for the Junior and Senior sections follow.
Through the yearParents–Teachers Meetings, Annual Sports Day, the Science and Technology Fair, the Annual Cultural Programme, national day functions and graduation.
