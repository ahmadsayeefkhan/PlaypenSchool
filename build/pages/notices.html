Every notice says when it applies.

Each notice here says what it is about, which topic it belongs to and the period it covers. When that period ends, the notice stays on the page and is marked as expired, so a family that saw it once can find it again.

Notices for a single class or section, such as the timings of a Parents–Teachers Meeting, are issued through the parent and student portal. Examination schedules and closures are announced there as well as here.
