Homepage media in assets/media/ (media producer E2, 2026-10-09)

How every file was made. tools/pipeline.py (stills), tools/video.py (both videos) and tools/media_list.py (the file table at the end), all run with python -I from the project folder. They read pixels only, from site/assets/source/playpen/ and the two Higgsfield downloads under raw/ (untrusted data, never executed), and write only to site/assets/media/ (and assets/3d/medallion-1k.glb). Every still and both videos carry the one grade of final-spec.md 2.1, applied once: white balance from the bright near-neutral pixels pulled halfway to neutral (±6 % cap) then a warm tint towards 5,200 K, shadows lifted 4 %, highlights +2 % warm, saturation −8 % with hues 335°–55° (brick, maroon, terracotta) protected, vignette 4 %, no grain. Video frames reuse the gains of their first frame so nothing flickers. Rasters: AVIF q55 + WebP q78 + JPG q78 at the widths of spec 8.1, quality stepped down per file until the ceiling of 8.1 is met (floors AVIF 36, WebP 56, JPG 58). Every <picture> should list AVIF first: the AVIF set meets every ceiling except life-0-720.avif (44 KB against 40); the WebP fallbacks that stay over their ceiling at the floor are learning-1, life-0, life-7, ground-1, ground-1-alt and a few 960/720 plates (all listed in the table by size), the JPG fallbacks of the same files too. No EXIF or profile travels: every source is converted to sRGB on read and saved without metadata. Lettering sweep: every crop was viewed at 2× for Bangla or stray lettering; the only lettering left is the school's own English (the crest and PLAYPEN on the facade, the "ANNUAL CULTURAL PROGRAMME 2024" stage banner, the maroon PLAYPEN sash) and the signatures on the student's painting. Faces: the files marked "faces" below show identifiable minors and are for the private presentation only (spec 16.3).
