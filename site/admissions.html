Before you apply

What you are actually choosing.

Playpen is an English-medium school in Bashundhara following the Cambridge curriculum from Playgroup through to A Level. It has been teaching in Dhaka since 1977 and moved into its purpose-built campus in 2018.

For most families the decision comes down to a few practical questions: is the class my child needs open, what will they be taught, who will look after them, and how do I apply. Those are the questions this section answers — and the Admissions Office will answer anything it does not.

The process

Five steps from enquiry to enrolment.

Enquire

Visit the Front Office, email the school, or contact the Admissions Department directly to ask about availability in the class you need.

Collect or download the form

Forms can be collected from the Front Office or downloaded from this page. Read the instructions carefully before filling it in.

Submit the form and documents

Completed forms are submitted at the Front Office and are then sorted by the Admissions Department.

Assessment and interview

Assessment for PG and Nursery. Written tests in English, Bengali and Mathematics from KG I to Class IX. The Principal conducts interviews. Class XI admission is based on Mock Examination and O Level results.

Decision and enrolment

Application status is communicated by telephone. Payment is made online through a school-authorised bank.

Applications are subject to space

Playpen accepts applications based on available seats in each class. Applying early, and confirming availability by phone first, saves everyone time.

School uniform

What students wear.

There is no uniform for Playgroup and Nursery classes; children should simply be dressed in casual wear. Compulsory uniforms begin from KG I onwards.

From Class IV

Girls wear salwar-kameez. Boys wear full trousers.

Footwear, Class X–XII

White keds or sneakers with white socks.

Seasonal items

Red and black sweaters are available seasonally. Classes IX–XII may wear black sweaters — not hoodies or jackets.

Where to buy the uniform

Uniforms for KG I through Class XII must be tailored to school specifications at one of the two authorised branches.

Admissions FAQ

Questions we are asked most.

Which classes are open for admission?
Applications are accepted based on available space, and availability changes by class and by year. A Level admission for the academic year July 2026 – June 2027 is currently open. For every other class, please call the Admissions Office to confirm availability before submitting a form.
Where do I collect the admission form?
Forms can be collected from the Front Office, or downloaded as a PDF from the Download section of this page. There is one form covering Playgroup to Class IX, and a separate A Level form.
What documents do I need to submit?
A completed admission form, the child’s birth certificate, their immunisation record, and school report cards covering the past two years. The admission form itself is the authoritative list — read it in full before submitting.
Is there an entrance test?
Yes, from KG I upwards. Applicants for PG and Nursery go through an assessment rather than a written test. From KG I to Class IX there are written tests in English, Bengali and Mathematics. The Principal conducts interviews. Class XI admission is based on Mock Examination results together with O Level results.
How will I know the outcome?
Application status is communicated by telephone, so please make sure the contact number on the form is one you can be reached on.
How are fees paid?
Payment is made online through a school-authorised bank. Digital fee payment has been mandatory since the 2021–2022 academic year, and the portal supports EBL (Visa and Mastercard), DBBL, Nagad, bKash, Rocket, Upay and Epay.
Is transport available?
Yes. The school has operated its own bus service since March 2014. Parents seeking bus service submit an application at the Administrative Office.
Is there a uniform?
There is no uniform for Playgroup and Nursery — casual wear is fine. Compulsory uniform begins from KG I. See the uniform section above for details and the two authorised tailoring branches.
What are the school office hours?
The Admissions Office can be reached between 9:00 AM and 1:00 PM. The school bookshop is open 8:30 AM to 1:00 PM.
How are A Level subjects chosen?
A Level students take a minimum of three subjects from the nine offered. Subject combinations are discussed with the Senior Vice-Principal and Teacher In-Charge, who also advise on university requirements. See the subject lists for what is currently offered.
Where is the school?
House 545/A, Road 19, Block J, Bashundhara Residential Area, Dhaka 1229. There is a map on the contact page.
