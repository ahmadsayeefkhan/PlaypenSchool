Contact

Call the school, or come and see it.

A telephone line for each section of the school, one address in Bashundhara R/A, and a short form that checks what you type and shows which number to call. The admissions lines are open 9.00 am to 1.00 pm.

01TelephoneOne line for each section of the school

Call the line for your child's section.

The school is reached by telephone or in person. Choose the line for the section your child is in, or would join: the first three are the admissions lines, open between 9.00 am and 1.00 pm.

The further-queries lines are the school's general numbers, and the ones to use in an emergency.

  • Playgroup to Class I01755 689 482
  • Classes II to VI01755 515 893
  • Classes VII to XII01755 693 623
  • Further queries and emergencies01755 515 885·+880 9678 434241
02A questionA prototype form: nothing is sent

Not sure which line to call?

Say what your question is about. The form checks your answers and shows the number to call.

This form is a prototype. It checks what you type, then shows the school's numbers. Nothing is sent, and the school does not receive your message.
