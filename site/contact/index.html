Contact

Call the school, or come and see it.

One telephone line for each section of the school, open 9.00 am to 1.00 pm. The address, the map, and how to ask a question, book a visit or come in are below.

02Ask, book or come inTwo forms that send, and the Front Office; each says what happens next

Ask, book, or come in.

Two forms on this website send to the admissions office and give you a code to quote when you call; the third way is to come to the Front Office.

  • Ask a question

    ENQ- code

    Enquire sends your question to the admissions office and gives you an ENQ- code. Choose your child's class and the page shows the line to call.

    Enquire

  • Book a visit

    VIS- code

    Book a visit sends your request and gives you a VIS- code. Come and see the building and the section your child would join; the office confirms the day.

    Book a visit

  • Come to the Front Office

    In person

    Completed admission forms are handed in at the Front Office, and the Admission Department sorts them. The admissions lines are open 9.00 am to 1.00 pm.

    How to apply

While this website is a sample, the two forms reach a demonstration desk, not the school's office, so please also call the line the form shows.

Visiting
To arrange a visit, call the admissions line for your child's section between 9.00 am and 1.00 pm, or book a visit online. The office confirms the day.
By car
Drivers are asked to follow the school's traffic rules, avoid the horn and slow down near the premises.
School bus
The school has run its own bus service since March 2014. Parents apply at the Administrative Office. Transport and safety has the rest.

The pin, the road and the block are set from the address the school publishes; the Google Maps link sits beside the address above. The school has not published landmarks or a visitors' gate, so none is drawn.
