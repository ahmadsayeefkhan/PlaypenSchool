Who leads the school, and who looks after each class.

A Chairman and a Managing Director, a Principal, two Vice Principals and six Teachers-in-Charge. The Vice Principals and Teachers-in-Charge look after assigned classes, supervising academic and administrative matters under the Principal's coordination.
