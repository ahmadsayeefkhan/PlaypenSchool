Page 01 of 07About

From a kindergarten to a school with its own ten-storey building.

Founded in 1977 by the educationist Mrs Zeba Khan. The first O Level candidate sat the examination in 2003, A Level classes began in 2013, and on 19 July 2018 the school moved into the building where it teaches today.

The building from the lawn, 2021.

01The recordEvery date once, in three eras

The school in three eras.

A kindergarten, then a Cambridge school, then a building of its own. The dates are the school's; the pictures are of the building as it is now, each dated under it.

  1. Beginnings, to 1998
  2. Cambridge, 2003 to 2015
  3. The building, 2014 to today

Begun as a kindergarten.

Playpen was founded in Dhaka by the educationist Mrs Zeba Khan. The school's own history describes it as starting with a kindergarten and growing class by class.

  1. Founded 1977

    A kindergarten in Dhaka

    The educationist Mrs Zeba Khan founds the school, which begins as a kindergarten.†

  2. 1998

    Primary classes

    By 1998 the school has added primary classes. Secondary classes follow; the school's own history gives no year for them.

O Level first, then A Level.

Since 2013 a child who joins in Playgroup can stay at Playpen until the end of school. The Cambridge pathway explains the examination years.

  1. 2003

    The first O Level candidate

    The school's first Ordinary Level candidate sits the examination and earns A grades in all seven subjects.

  2. 2013

    A Level classes begin

    Chairman Mr A. Mannan Khan introduces A Level. The school now teaches from Playgroup to A Level.

  3. 2015

    The first A Level batch

    The first full batch of students enrols for the two-year course. A Level teaching had begun in 2013.

And today: the hall doors dressed for Graduation, 2024. The ceremony is for the O and A Level leavers of the year.

Into a building of its own.

Principal Mrs Sorabon Tohura led the move. Chairman Mr A. Mannan Khan oversaw the construction, and the architect was Mr Ehsan Khan. Our campus walks through the building.

  1. March 2014

    The school's own buses

    The school begins to run its own bus service. Transport and safety says how a family applies for a place.

  2. 19 July 2018

    The move to Bashundhara

    Under Principal Mrs Sorabon Tohura, the school moves into its own building of ten storeys in Bashundhara Residential Area. The architect is Mr Ehsan Khan.

And today: the crest on the street front of the building, 2024.

The second phase of the building is in progress. Where the school is now, below, says what it adds.

03The school todayWhere the story has got to

Where the school is now.

  • 01

    Four sections, Playgroup to Class XII

    Elementary, Junior, Middle and Senior, with Cambridge O Level, AS and A Level at the top.

  • 02

    One building of ten storeys, since July 2018

    Classrooms, laboratories, the library, the hall, the field and the roof, in Bashundhara.

  • 03

    Two Vice Principals and six Teachers-in-Charge

    Every class has a person who looks after it, and a line to call.
