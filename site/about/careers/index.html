Teaching at Playpen, and how to apply.

Four facts about the school you would join, the school's own words about its teachers, and one route to send an application.

A corner of the terrace, 2024.

01The school you would joinFour facts and the school's own words

Why teach at Playpen.

Nothing here is a promise. These are facts already on the school's pages, and one sentence the school says about its own staff.

  • A Cambridge school

    O Level, AS and A Level

    Since 2020 the school has been a Cambridge exam venue, approved by the British Council. Students sit their exams in the school's own hall.

  • One purpose-built building

    Ten storeys, since July 2018

    Air-conditioned classrooms, and separate laboratories for Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Computer Science.

    Our campus
  • Playgroup to Class XII

    Four sections, two semesters

    A day school in Bashundhara, with no boarding. A written examination ends each semester.

    Learning at Playpen
  • Long-serving colleagues

    Many Faculty Members are serving here for as long as 30-40 years.

    From the careers page of the school's website. The statement, and its spelling, are the school's own.

Every year, Playpen students receive The Daily Star Awards: for six or more A grades at O Level, and for three or more A grades at A Level.

Results and achievements

From the school's results summary for the May to June 2026 exam session, and the school's careers, campus and admissions pages, September 2026.

02ApplyOne route

Send an application.

No vacancies are listed today. The school reads every application and contacts candidates when an opening matches their qualifications.

Fill in the form, add your CV and a photograph, and press Send. You get a reference code at once.

03After you applyWhat happens next

What happens next.

The school's reply

Online, you get a reference code at once; by hand, the office tells you how your CV was received. The school contacts candidates when an opening matches their qualifications. No reply time is published.
