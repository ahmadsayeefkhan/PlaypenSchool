Page 04 of 07About

A purpose-built building of ten storeys, in Bashundhara.

The school has taught here since 19 July 2018, from Playgroup to Class XII, in a building designed by the architect Mr Ehsan Khan.

The building from the field, January 2020

Playpen is a day school, with no boarding. Every class from Playgroup to Class XII is taught in this one building.

The field and the courtyards.

A grass playground and open courtyards lie at the foot of the building. Annual Sports Day is held on the field, and Pohela Boishakh fills the courtyard.

The courtyard between the wings, from above, 2024.

On the field
Annual Sports Day, and the games the school plays. Sports lists them.
Indoors
Table tennis, chess and carrom are played through the year.

The classrooms, the laboratories and the library.

Every class from Playgroup to Class XII is taught in this one building, in air-conditioned classrooms, with separate laboratories for Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Computer Science, and a library with the bookshop alongside.

The classroom floors from the street, January 2021.

The classrooms

Four sections
Elementary, Junior, Middle and Senior. Learning at Playpen says what each one teaches.
Who looks after a class
A Teacher-in-Charge for each band of classes, under a Vice Principal. Find your child's class.
Power
Generators keep classes running through an electricity failure.

The laboratories

Four laboratories
Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Computer Science, each in its own room. The Science Fair is one of the events in the school's year.
Electives
Students choose their O Level electives in Class VIII. Senior School lists them.

The library and the bookshop

Library classes
Run for every level, monitored by a Librarian. If a book is not returned, the Librarian tells the parents through the class teacher. Policies has the rule.
The bookshop
Parents collect the year's books and copies at the start of each session. Starting at Playpen has the details.

The hall and the activity rooms.

The multipurpose hall holds the school's gatherings, from assemblies to cultural programmes and prize-givings. It is also where students sit their Cambridge O and A Level exams.

The hall during the visit of NASA astronaut Joseph M. Acaba, December 2024.

The activity rooms
A students' gallery for group work and presentation, and rooms for art, music, dance, drama and clubs. Clubs and activities says what runs in them.
Exams
Since 2020 the school has been a Cambridge exam venue, approved by the British Council. Examinations and reporting

The terraces and the roof.

Brick terraces with benches and trees in planters, and the city beyond the railing. A basketball court sits on the roof.

The terrace between the two wings, 2024.

The rooftop swimming pool.

A swimming pool on the roof is on the school's list of facilities. The picture is an illustration until the school's photograph is ready.

Illustration, not a photograph of the school. A photograph of the pool will replace it.

Safety and health.

What the building has on every floor and round the clock, from the school's own list.

On the campus
  • CCTV across the campus, and security staff round the clock
  • A sick room for minor health problems during school hours; serious cases are handed to the parents
  • Drinking water, separate toilets for boys and girls, and fire extinguishers on every floor
  • An on-site cafeteria, which the second phase is to expand
  • Generators for classes during an electricity failure, and an internal telephone system between offices and floors
Child protection, and who to call with a concern

The school's own buses have run since March 2014; the rules for the bus and how to apply for a place are on Transport and safety.

In progress
