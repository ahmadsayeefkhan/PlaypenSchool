The people who run the school.

The Chairman and the Managing Director, the Principal, two Vice Principals and six Teachers-in-Charge. Each Vice Principal and Teacher-in-Charge looks after a band of classes, under the Principal.

Creepers over the street front of the school, 2024.

01The structureTitles as the school publishes them

How the school is organised.

Management, then the Principal, then two Vice Principals, each with the Teachers-in-Charge whose classes fall in her or his band. Someone is responsible for every class from Playgroup to Class XII.
