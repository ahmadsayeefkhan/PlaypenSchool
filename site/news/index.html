Reports from the school year.

Event reports and competition results, each with the date the school's own record gives. Reports for the current school year are published as the school writes them.

What the school has recorded.

Event reports

  • January 2026Event report

    Annual Sports Day 2026

    A display in red on the school field, seen from above, a march past, and athletics for the senior classes. The school's own photographs of the day come in two sets, one for Classes I to XII and one for Playgroup to KG II.

  • 2025Event report

    Graduation Ceremony 2025

    The Graduation Ceremony of Ordinary and Advanced Level, in the multipurpose hall: the graduates in caps, gowns and maroon sashes before a red stage.

  • April 2025Event report

    Pohela Boishakh 1432

    An alpona painted on the courtyard floor, stalls with clay pots and folk art, and dance for the Bengali new year.

  • December 2024Event report

    A NASA astronaut visits the school

    NASA astronaut Joseph M. Acaba spoke to the students in the multipurpose hall, which was full for the visit.

  • Nov2024

    Event report

    Science and Technology Fair 2024

    Project stalls, models and experiments in the hall, with students in laboratory coats at the stalls and judges moving between them.

  • 2024

    Event report

    Annual Cultural Programme 2024

    The school's annual performance event, staged in the multipurpose hall, with dance on stage and young children in traditional dress.

  • 2024

    Event report

    Playpen Interschool Football Tournament 2024

    Matches on the school's sand pitch, with the brick building behind and spectators along the edge.

Competition results

  • 27Apr 2024

    Result, olympiad

    1st Space Exploration Olympiad

    Organised by the Bangladesh Innovation Forum at AIUB. Two Playpen students were second runner-up.

  • 24Feb 2024

    Result, language

    13th Bangla Olympiad

    A Class VII student took first position in Essay Writing, and the school's group dance team also took first position.

  • 24Feb 2024

    Result, language

    Bakeman's 3rd International Language League

    Three Class IX students were runners-up for their presentation on Bangladeshi folk art.

  • 23Feb 2024

    Result, mathematics

    16th National Abacus and Mental Arithmetic Competition

    One Playpen student was third runner-up.

  • 9Feb 2024

    Result, olympiad

    Regional Physics Olympiad

    One Playpen student placed sixth in Category A.

  • Jan2024

    Result, mathematics and technology

    Math and Tech Fest

    Class VIII students took first position in Crypto Craft Chronicles. One student won first place in the Rube Goldberg competition and the campus ambassador award.

  • Jan2024

    Result, football

    Scholastica Inter School Football

    The school's list records two individual awards for Playpen players, a player award and a defender award.

  • 14Oct 2023

    Result, environment

    Eco-Schools Programme

    Three Class IX students were first runner-up in the presentation category.

  • 26Aug 2023

    Result, basketball

    Inter-House Basketball

    House Earth Boys won the boys' competition, and House Jupiter Girls won the girls'.

  • Aug2023

    Result, football

    Hurdco Inter School Football

    Playpen were runners-up.

  • 2023

    Result, language

    International Spell Bee Competition

    A Class I student received an Appreciation Medal, as did a second student. Three further students earned participation certificates.

About these reports

Students are not named here. The school's own list names them, and a name will appear on this site only once the school has confirmed the family's consent. A date is shown as the school's record gives it: where the record names a month or a year only, that is all this page shows.
