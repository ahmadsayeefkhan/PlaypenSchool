The whole of a childhood, in one school.

Playgroup to A Level, under one roof. An English-medium day school with Cambridge O Level, AS and A Level, and nearly 2,000 students on one campus in Dhaka.

Sheet 01 of 08 Ground The school in one sheet

The school in one sheet.

Playpen is a non-residential day school: every child goes home in the afternoon. Since July 2018 it has lived in its own ten-storey building of brick and concrete in Bashundhara R/A, and a child can go from Playgroup to Class XII without ever changing school.

Sheet 04 of 08 School life The year, in the courtyard and on the field

Pohela Boishakh, painted on the courtyard floor.

In April 2025 the courtyard was laid with an alpona for Pohela Boishakh, the Bengali new year, and the school came in red and white. The same ground holds Annual Sports Day; the hall holds the Science and Technology Fair, the Cultural Programme and the graduation.

A year at Playpen.

Clubs run from Art and Craft, Music and Dance to Drama, Debate, the Olympiads and the Duke of Edinburgh's Award. Teams play football, basketball, handball, athletics, table tennis, chess and carrom.

Sheet 08 of 08 Admissions Visit, enquire, apply

Visit, enquire, apply.

Applications for the current year are accepted if there is available space.

  1. 01

    Call or visit.

    Admissions take calls between 9.00 am and 1.00 pm. Call the line for your child's section, or come and see the building.

  2. 02

    Collect the form.

    From the Front Office, or download it from the school's site.

  3. 03

    Assessment or test.

    Playgroup and Nursery sit an assessment. KG I to Class IX sit a written test in English, Bengali and Mathematics. Class XI admission is based on the school's mock examinations and the O Level result.

  4. 04

    Interview.

    With the Principal and the parents.

  5. 05

    The result.

    By telephone. Fees and monthly tuition are explained after selection and paid online through the school's bank.

What to bring

  • Completed form
  • Birth certificate
  • Immunisation record
  • Report cards for the last two years
