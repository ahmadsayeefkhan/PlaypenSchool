Sheet 02 of 09 Achievement Awards and firsts

01/07Graduation Ceremony 2025

Honoured every year.

Every year, Playpen students receive The Daily Star Awards: for six or more A grades at O Level, and for three or more A grades at A Level.

The record goes back to 2003, when the school’s first O Level candidate earned straight A grades in all seven subjects. Many former students have since gone on to study at universities abroad.

  • 6+6 or more A grades at O Level for a Daily Star Award
  • 3+3 or more A grades at A Level for a Daily Star Award
  • 7/77 out of 7 subjects at grade A for the first O Level candidate, 2003

Sheet 05 of 09 Events Through the school year

The big days of the school year.

Five events the whole school looks forward to, on the field, in the courtyard and in the hall.

  • 01 Annual Sports Day Students take to the field for races, displays and the march past, with families watching. About Sports Day
  • 02 Pohela Boishakh The Bengali new year: an alpona painted on the courtyard floor, music, dance and stalls. About Pohela Boishakh
  • 03 Science and Technology Fair Students set up experiments and models in the hall and explain them to visitors. About the fair
  • 04 Annual Cultural Programme Song, dance and drama on stage, performed by the students. About the programme
  • 05 Graduation Ceremony Leavers in cap and gown are honoured in the hall, with their families there to see it. About graduation

The year also brings Victory Day, Independence Day, Shaheed Dibosh, the Book Fair, the Education Fair, Business Week, the Annual Drama, Orientation Day, class parties and a study tour.

Sheet 09 of 09 Admissions Visit, enquire, apply

Visit, enquire, apply.

Applications for the current year are accepted if there is available space.

  1. 01

    Call or visit.

    Admissions take calls between 9.00 am and 1.00 pm. Call the line for your child's section, or come and see the building.

  2. 02

    Collect the form.

    From the Front Office, or download it from the school's site.

  3. 03

    Assessment or test.

    Playgroup and Nursery sit an assessment. KG I to Class IX sit a written test in English, Bengali and Mathematics. Class XI admission is based on the school's mock examinations and the O Level result.

  4. 04

    Interview.

    With the Principal and the parents.

  5. 05

    The result.

    By telephone. Fees and monthly tuition are explained after selection and paid online through the school's bank.

What to bring

  • Completed form
  • Birth certificate
  • Immunisation record
  • Report cards for the last two years
