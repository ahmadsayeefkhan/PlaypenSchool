Getting to school, and how the campus is kept safe.

The school has run its own buses since March 2014. This page sets out how to apply for a place, the rules on the bus and at the gate, and what the campus has in place for safety.

02The school busThe school's own service

Asking for a place on the school bus.

Playpen has run its own bus service since March 2014, and the school says it puts student safety and welfare first. Parents apply for a place.

  1. 01

    Apply at the Administrative Office.

    Parents who want a bus place for their child submit an application at the Administrative Office.

  2. 02

    The office confirms route and place.

    Routes and availability are confirmed by the office when the application is made.

  3. 03

    Go through the bus rules with your child.

    The rules are set out below. They apply to every student who travels on the school bus.

Not published

The school has not published its routes, the areas it serves, the number of buses, whether an attendant rides on each bus, whether the buses are tracked, or the transport fee. Please ask at the Administrative Office when you apply.

03On the bus and at the gateThe school's rules

What the school asks of students and drivers.

Students on the bus

  • Listen to and respect teacher instructions
  • Speak quietly
  • Do not eat or drink on board
  • Keep hands inside the bus
  • Use appropriate language

Arrival and departure

  • Students are asked to carry their own bag instead of using the luggage compartment: it reduces congestion and lateness at the gate
  • Drivers are asked to follow the school's traffic rules and to avoid using the horn
  • Drivers are asked to slow down near the school premises

05Everyday safetyWhat parents are asked to do

Small things that keep a day safe.

Identity cards
Every student receives an identity card on admission and wears it every day on school premises. A lost or damaged card is replaced through the school office.
Contact details
Parents tell teachers and the office, in writing, whenever an address, phone number, email address or emergency contact changes.
Mobile phones
The code of conduct does not allow mobile phones for students up to Class XII. A student who needs to reach a parent urgently can do so through the school office.
A concern about a child
Raise it with the class teacher, the Teacher-in-Charge for the class, or the school office. The numbers are below.
A counsellor
A student counsellor, a designated member of the faculty, is on standby. Student support has more.
06QuestionsAbout the bus

Questions parents ask about the bus.

Does the school run its own buses?

Yes. The school has run its own bus service since March 2014. Parents who want a place apply at the Administrative Office.

Which areas do the buses serve?

The school has not published its routes. The Administrative Office confirms the route, and whether a place is free, when you apply.

What does a bus place cost?

The transport fee is not published on this site. Please ask at the Administrative Office when you apply. Fees and payment explains how the school's other fees are given and paid.

Is there an attendant on the bus, or are the buses tracked?

The school has not said whether an attendant rides on each bus or whether the buses are tracked. Please ask at the Administrative Office before you decide.

Can my child bring a phone on the bus?

The school's code of conduct does not allow mobile phones for students up to Class XII. Policies and documents has the code of conduct.
