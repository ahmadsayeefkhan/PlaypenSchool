02On stageWhat the year's performances build towards

Music, dance and drama.

Music, both instrumental and vocal, Dance and Drama run as regular activities. The building has rooms set aside for art, music, dance, drama and club activity, and Drama is staged in the multipurpose hall.

The hall is also where the Annual Cultural Programme takes place, the school's largest performance event; the year's music, dance and drama build towards it. Read about the Cultural Programme.

On 24 February 2024 the school's group dance team took first position at the 13th Bangla Olympiad.

03In the studioArt and Craft, in the pupils' own work

Art and Craft.

Art and Craft is on the school's list of activities. Its own album shows paper craft, clay models of houses and forts, and miniature gardens set out on the terrace steps.

Students who want to take art further can choose Art & Design as one of their O Level electives. Senior School lists the choices.

The Art and Craft album

04Science, fairs and workshopsWhat the school holds, and the topics it lists for seniors

The Science Fair, and workshops for seniors.

The school holds science fairs and enters students in fairs outside it. At its Science and Technology Fair, one of the events the school names as annual, students run stalls of projects, experiments and models in the multipurpose hall. The most recent album is from 2024.

Workshops and seminars run through the year, mainly for senior students. The school lists these topics:

  • Internet safety and responsible social media
  • University applications to international institutions
  • Model United Nations
  • United World College programmes
  • The Duke of Edinburgh's Award

Visitors come too. In December 2024 NASA astronaut Joseph M. Acaba spoke to students in the multipurpose hall, and a student painted his portrait.
