School life

The year, in the courtyard and on the field.

Five pages: the school day, the way to school and the gate, sport, clubs and activities, and the Cultural Programme and community service. The fixtures of the year, with their albums, are on Events.

01At a glanceThree figures from the pages below

Seven games, four dates in the national calendar, thirteen activities.

  • 7games: football, basketball, handball, athletics, table tennis, chess and carrom
  • 4days in the national calendar the school marks: 21 February, 26 March, 14 April and 16 December
  • 13activities from the school's activities and events pages, from Art and Craft to the Duke of Edinburgh's Award

The Cultural Programme and community service run beside these through the year; the school day, the gate and the school's own buses have their pages below too.

Source: the school's sports, activities and events pages.

02The five pagesEach card opens its page

Where to look first.

The practical two first, for families settling in; then sport, clubs and the year's festivals. Each card opens its page.

  • 01 The school day The shape of a school day, from the gate to the gate, and the four practical things new families ask about first. About the school day
  • 02 Transport and safety What the campus has in place for safety, the rules at the gate, and the school's own buses, which have run since March 2014. About transport and safety
  • 03 Sports Seven games, teams for boys and for girls, and Annual Sports Day on the field. About sports
  • 04 Clubs and activities Art and Craft, Music, Dance, Drama, Debate, the Olympiads, the Duke of Edinburgh's Award and more. About clubs and activities
  • 05 Culture and service The Cultural Programme, Pohela Boishakh in the courtyard, the national days, and community service. About culture and service

The fixtures of the year, each with its page and its album, are on Events. Dates for the coming year come from the school: it announces them as notices and publishes them on the portal with the academic calendar.

Houses

The school has houses that compete on Sports Day and in tournaments such as inter-house basketball. Two are named in its published results, House Earth and House Jupiter.
