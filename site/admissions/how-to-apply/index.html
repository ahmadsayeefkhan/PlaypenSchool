How to apply

02The five stepsAs the school runs them

From the first call to the result.

  1. 01

    Collect the form.

    Take a printed form from the Front Office, or download it from the school's website. There is one form for Playgroup to Class IX and a separate form for AS and A Level. The forms are listed below.

  2. 02

    Hand it in.

    Take the completed form, with the documents under What to bring, to the Front Office. The Admission Department sorts the forms.

  3. 03

    Assessment or test.

    Once the Principal has approved the form, the school calls the parents for the assessment or test. Playgroup and Nursery children sit an assessment. From KG I to Class IX the child sits a written test in English, Bengali and Mathematics. For Class XI, admission is based on the school's mock examinations and the O Level result.

  4. 04

    Meet the Principal.

    After the assessment or test, the child and the parents have an interview with the Principal. The Principal looks at the test result and the interview, then makes the final decision. The school describes this for Playgroup to Class IX, and describes no interview for Class XI.

  5. 05

    Hear the result.

    The result comes by telephone. Make sure the number on the form is one on which you can be reached.

After selection

Once a child has been selected, the school explains the admission fee and the monthly tuition. Payment is made online, through the school's authorised bank. Fees and payment

The Playpen School, Dhaka: what to bring with the admission form.

What to bring

  • The completed admission form
  • The child's birth certificate
  • The immunisation record
  • Report cards for the last two years

Read the form in full before you hand it in. If your child has not been at school before, ask the admissions line which of these apply.

04Dates and timingWhat the school has not published

When can we apply?

Applications for the current year are accepted if there is available space. The school has not published opening or closing dates, a deadline or the age for each class.

Space changes by class and by year, so call the line for your child's section before you hand in a form. Lines are open 9.00 am to 1.00 pm.
