What is it about?

Space in a class

Applications for the current year are accepted if there is space, and space changes by class and by year. The quickest answer is the line for your child's section, above. If you write, say the class and the year.

The test

Playgroup and Nursery children sit an assessment. From KG I to Class IX the child sits a written test in English, Bengali and Mathematics. Class XI admission is based on the school's mock examinations and the O Level result. How to apply has the five steps.

Fees

The school explains the admission fee and the monthly tuition after a child has been selected. Amounts come from the admissions office, not from this site. Fees and payment explains how fees are paid.

A visit

Call first; the office confirms the day. Ask whether you can see the building and the section your child would join. Book a visit has a form and a list of questions to take along.

Transport

The school has run its own bus service since March 2014. Parents who want a place apply at the Administrative Office; routes are not published, so ask whether the bus serves your area. Transport and safety

Uniform and books

There is no uniform for Playgroup and Nursery; from KG I the uniform is compulsory and made at the school's tailoring house. Books and exercise copies come from the school bookshop. Starting at Playpen
