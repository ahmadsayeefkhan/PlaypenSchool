Admissions

Visit, enquire, apply.

Applications for the current year are accepted if there is available space. Call the line for your child's section, visit the school, then apply.

02The first stepsVisit, enquire, apply

Look, ask, then apply.

  1. 01

    Visit.

    Visit the school with your questions. Call the admissions line for your child's section first, between 9.00 am and 1.00 pm.

    Book a visit

  2. 02

    Enquire.

    Ask what a website cannot say: whether a class has space, which form to use, what to bring. The admissions team answers on the line for your child's section.

    Enquire

  3. 03

    Apply.

    Collect the form and hand it in at the Front Office. The school then assesses your child, and the result comes by telephone.

    How to apply

03Entry pointsThree ways in

Which class is your child joining?

The school looks at a child in a different way at each entry point: an assessment for Playgroup and Nursery, a written test from KG I, and the school's mock examinations and the O Level result for Class XI.

  • Entry point 01

    Playgroup and Nursery

    How the child is assessed
    An assessment
    Form
    Admission form, Playgroup to Class IX
    After that
    An interview with the Principal and the parents, then the result by telephone

    How to apply for Playgroup or Nursery

  • Entry point 02

    KG I to Class IX

    How the child is assessed
    A written test in English, Bengali and Mathematics
    Form
    Admission form, Playgroup to Class IX
    After that
    An interview with the Principal and the parents, then the result by telephone

    How to apply for KG I to Class IX

  • Entry point 03

    Class XI, AS Level

    How the child is assessed
    The school's mock examinations and the O Level result
    Form
    A Level admission form
    After that
    The result by telephone. The school describes no interview for this entry point

    How to apply for Class XI

The school's procedure does not describe entry to Class X or Class XII. For those classes, call 01755 693 623.

04Who to callLines are open 9.00 am to 1.00 pm

Call the line for your child's section.

Lines are open 9.00 am to 1.00 pm. Space changes by class and by year, so a call before you apply saves a form.

  • Classes II to VI01755 515 893
