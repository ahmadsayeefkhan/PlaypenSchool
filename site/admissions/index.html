01Your child's classChoose once; every admissions page remembers it

Start with your child's class.

The school looks at a child in a different way at each class: an assessment for Playgroup and Nursery, a written test from KG I, and the mock examinations and the O Level result for Class XI. Choose the class, and this page shows the test, the form and the line to call.

Which class is your child joining?

    The line for your child's section, in the call sheet: Playgroup to Class I for KG I and Class I; Classes II to VI; Classes VII to XII.

    How to apply for KG I to Class IX

  • Class XI, AS Level

    How the child is assessed
    The school's mock examinations and the O Level result. There is no written entrance test.
    The form
    A Level admission form. Download it.
    After that
    The result by telephone. There is no interview for Class XI.
    Which line to call
    The Classes VII to XII line, in the call sheet.

    How to apply for Class XI

  • Class X and Class XII

    Ask the line

    The school's procedure describes entry to Playgroup to Class IX and to Class XI, not to Class X or Class XII. Call the Classes VII to XII line, in the call sheet, and ask whether a place is possible.

02The stepsAs the school's procedure runs them

Five steps, one telephone call at the end.

The same five steps for every class. Only the third one changes with the class, and the card above has already told you which.

  1. 01

    Call or visit.

    Call the line for your child's section, between 9.00 am and 1.00 pm, or come to the Front Office with your questions.

    Book a visit

  2. 02

    Collect and hand in the form.

    From the Front Office, as the school's PDF, or filled in online on this site and printed. Hand it in at the Front Office with the documents asked for.

    Get the form

  3. 03

    Assessment or test.

    An assessment for Playgroup and Nursery. A written test in English, Bengali and Mathematics from KG I to Class IX. The mock examinations and the O Level result for Class XI.

    The five steps in detail

  4. 04

    Meet the Principal.

    After the assessment or test, the child and the parents meet the Principal, who makes the decision. Playgroup to Class IX; there is no interview for Class XI.

    The test and the interview

  5. 05

    Hear the result.

    By telephone, on the number you gave on the form. The school explains the fees after selection.

    Fees and payment

What to bring with the form: the child's birth certificate, the immunisation record and report cards for the last two years.
