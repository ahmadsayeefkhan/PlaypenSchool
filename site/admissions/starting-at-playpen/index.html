02UniformBy class, and where to have it made

What children wear.

Nothing until KG I; a uniform and sportswear from then on. The rules change at Class IV and again for the senior classes.

  • Playgroup and Nursery

    No uniform. Children wear casual clothes.

  • KG I to Class III

    The school uniform and sportswear, both compulsory.

  • Class IV onwards

    Girls wear salwar-kameez. Boys wear full trousers.

  • Classes IX to XII

    As above. Black sweaters are allowed in winter; hoodies and jackets are not.

For every class

Footwear: white keds or sneakers with white socks, for all students and especially Classes X to XII. Sweaters: red and black sweaters are available at the school in the winter season. Sportswear: bought from the school, worn on the days a class has Games.

Where to have the uniform made

From KG I, uniforms are made to the school's specifications at its tailoring house, which has two branches.

  • Gulshan branch

    Pladium Market, Shop 19, Gulshan-2

  • Bashundhara branch

    Rupayan Shopping Square, Shop 310, Bashundhara

Both branches' telephone numbers are on the school's own uniform page; they will be printed here once the school confirms them.

The class bands are the school's own: KG I, Class IV and Classes IX to XII. No colours are published for the uniform itself, so none are given here.

From the school's uniform page, re-read on 11 October 2026.

03Books and the bookshopThe school's own shop

Books, copies and sportswear come from the school.

The school has its own bookshop. Parents collect the yearly books and exercise copies there at the beginning of each session.

Bookshop hours: 8.30 am until 1.00 pm. The days are not published, so ask.

Sportswear is bought from the school as well. There is a cafeteria on the campus.

04The practical thingsBus, card, portal, fees, rules, the year

Six things to know.

The school bus
The school has run its own bus service since March 2014. Parents who want a place apply at the Administrative Office. Transport and safety has the details.
The identity card
Every student receives an identity card after admission and wears it every day on school premises. A lost or damaged card must be replaced.
The portal
Parents and students use the portal for academic performance, class tests, homework, report cards and attendance. Portalcurrent site (opens in a new tab)
Paying fees
Fees are paid online. Fees and payment explains how. Pay feescurrent site (opens in a new tab)
School rules
The code of conduct covers mobile phones, which are not allowed for students up to Class XII, as well as absence and contact details. Read it on Policies and documents.
The school year
The year has two semesters, each ending with a written examination. Dates are published through the school's notices and the portal. See Events and term dates, Notices and The school day.

The identity card rules, the portal login and the first-week routine are not published; the school's pages say only what is here.

Last reviewed 11 October 2026, against the school's own pages.
