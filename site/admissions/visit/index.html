01Before you comeWhat the school has described

A visit starts with a call.

The school takes enquiries and hands out admission forms at its Front Office. Three steps get you there with the right questions.

  1. 01

    Choose your child's class.

    Each section has its own admissions line. The form below shows the line for the class you choose, and remembers it on the other admissions pages.

  2. 02

    Call, or send the form.

    The office calls back to confirm a day. The school has not published visit days, so no day is promised here.

  3. 03

    Come to the Front Office.

    House 545/A, Block J, Road 19, Bashundhara R/A, Dhaka 1229. Map (opens Google Maps in a new tab)

When the office calls

Ask whether you can see the building and the section your child would join, and how long to allow. Bring your questions: there is a list below.
