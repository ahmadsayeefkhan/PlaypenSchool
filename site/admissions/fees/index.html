How the fees work.

The school explains the admission fee and the monthly tuition after a child has been selected. Fees are paid online through the school's bank. Amounts come from the admissions office.

01How fees workIn the order they come

Three steps, after selection.

The office explains the fees once a child has a place, then payment runs online.

  1. 01

    The result comes by telephone.

    Once the admission process is complete, the school calls the number you gave on the form.

  2. 02

    The office explains the fees.

    The admission fee, the monthly tuition and the payment details, for your child's class.

  3. 03

    You pay online.

    Through a bank the school authorises. Online payment has been required since the 2021 to 2022 session. Ask the office for the day of the month it falls due.

Tuition is monthly. The list below gives you the questions to ask about what it covers.
