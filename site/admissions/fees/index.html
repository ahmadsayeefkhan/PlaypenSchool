Fees and payment

The school explains the admission fee and the monthly tuition after a child has been selected, and fees are paid online through its bank. This page sets out how that works, and says plainly what the school has not published.

01How fees workIn the order they come

Fees are explained after selection.

  1. 01

    Your child is selected.

    The school gives the result by telephone once the admission process is complete.

  2. 02

    The school explains the fees.

    After selection, the school briefs the family on the admission fee and the monthly tuition. Its Admission Department deals with the banking formalities.

  3. 03

    Payment is made online.

    Payment goes through a bank the school authorises. The school has required online payment of fees since the 2021 to 2022 session.

  4. 04

    Tuition is monthly.

    The school describes the tuition as monthly. It has not published the date on which each month's tuition falls due, so ask when you call.

02The amountsNot published by the school

The school has not published its fees.

This page does not guess at the amounts. To ask before you apply, call the line for your child's section, between 9.00 am and 1.00 pm.

03Before you decideQuestions to put to the school

Questions worth asking when you call.

  1. What the admission fee is, and when it is paid.
  2. The monthly tuition for your child's class.
  3. What the tuition covers.
  4. Whether uniform, books, exercise copies, sportswear and the bus are charged separately.
  5. How refunds and late payments are handled.
  6. Whether the fees change from one year to the next.

04PayingOnline, through the school's bank

Fees are paid online.

Online payment has been required since the 2021 to 2022 session. Among the channels the school lists are:

  • EBL, Visa and Mastercard
  • DBBL
  • Nagad
  • bKash
  • Rocket
  • Upay
  • Epay

The school's own list ends with "and many more".

A question about a payment

Call the line for your child's section, or the further queries line, 01755 515 885.

05School itemsWhere families get them

Uniform, books, sportswear and the bus.

Uniform
Compulsory from KG I. It is made to the school's specifications at its tailoring house, which has a Gulshan branch and a Bashundhara branch.
Sportswear
Bought from the school.
Books and exercise copies
From the school bookshop. Parents collect the yearly books and copies at the beginning of each session.
The school bus
Parents who want a place apply at the Administrative Office.

The school's pages do not say whether any of these is part of the fees, so ask when you call. The details are on Starting at Playpen and Transport and safety.
