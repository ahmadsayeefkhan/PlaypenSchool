The Playpen Alumni Association.

The school wants to hear from former students, wherever they are now. This page says how to send your details, and what the school has not yet published.

01The associationWho it is for

Keeping in touch with former students.

The Playpen Alumni Association collects the details of former students, with a recent photograph. The school says it wishes to connect with students who have excelled in their fields, wherever in the world that has taken them.

The form below is a draft of what to send: your name, your class and the year you left, how to reach you, and a memory of the school if you wish to give one.

The school has published little about its former students so far: no alumni events and no named alumni. The sections below say where each of those stands.

03The school across the yearsFrom the school's own record

Dates to place your year.

A few dates from the school's own record. Earlier years are on the History page.

  1. 2003

    The first O Level candidate

    Playpen enters its first candidate for the Ordinary Level examination, who takes top marks in seven subjects.

  2. 2013

    A Level classes begin

    The school completes the path from Playgroup to A Level.

  3. March 2014

    The school's own buses

    The school begins to run its own bus service.

  4. 19 July 2018

    The move to the new building

    The school moves into its own building in Bashundhara R/A, where it teaches today.

  5. 2025

    Graduation of O Level and A Level students

    The ceremony is held in the multipurpose hall, with the graduates in caps, gowns and maroon sashes.
