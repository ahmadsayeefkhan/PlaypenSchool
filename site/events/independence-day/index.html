Independence Day.

26 March. The national day of Bangladesh, which declared its independence on this day in 1971.

Independence Day at a glance

Kind of event
National days
When
26 MarchEvery year; a public holiday. The school announces how it marks the day.
02What happensThe day, in short

The national day, marked with a function.

Independence Day, on 26 March, is the national day of Bangladesh. It marks the declaration of independence in 1971, at the start of the Liberation War.

Playpen marks Independence Day with a school function each year. Its gallery holds an album of the art competition held for the day.

Read more

  • The days the school marks

The school's album "Independence Day Art Competition" holds 11 photographs. The painted panels at the top of this page are the one frame in which no child can be recognised; the rest are on the school's current website and come here when the school agrees in writing.
