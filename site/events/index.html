The school year, event by event.

Fifteen events the school holds every year, each with a page of its own. Four fall on fixed dates; the school announces the rest, as notices and on the parent portal.

01The big daysFive events most families know

The days the whole school comes together.

Five days a year the whole school is in one place: on the field, in the courtyard, in the hall.

  • SportsAnnounced each year

    Annual Sports Day

    The march past, the displays and the races, with every class on the school field.

  • Culture14 April

    Pohela Boishakh

    The Bengali new year, with an alpona in the courtyard, folk art and dance.

  • Illustration

    AcademicAnnounced each year

    Science and Technology Fair

    Student projects, experiments and models at stalls in the hall.

  • CultureAnnounced each year

    Annual Cultural Programme

    The school's largest performance event: music, dance and drama.

  • AcademicAnnounced each year

    Graduation Ceremony

    The O Level and A Level graduates, in caps, gowns and maroon sashes.

Every one of these five days happens on the school's own field, in its courtyard or in its multipurpose hall.

02The yearTwo semesters, July to June

July to June, in two semesters.

The first semester runs July to December and the second January to June. A written examination closes each one. Only the national days have fixed dates; the school announces every other event, as a notice and on the parent portal.

First semester

July to December
  • Teaching, class tests, class work marking and homework, all followed on the portal.
  • A formal written examination at the end; progress reports follow.

Second semester

January to June
  • Teaching continues. Class VIII students choose their O Level elective subjects.
  • A formal written examination at the end; promotion decisions follow.

Through the year

Both semesters
  • Parents-Teachers Meetings, announced at suitable intervals.
  • The events on this page, each announced by the school.

Examinations

Dates reach families as notices from the school; urgent notices also go by SMS and email; the academic calendar and upcoming events are on the parent portal.

03Every eventFifteen, in four kinds

Fifteen events, four kinds.

The five days above, and ten more, each with a page of its own.

Academic7

  • Science and Technology FairAnnounced each yearStudent projects, experiments and models at stalls in the hall.
  • Graduation CeremonyAnnounced each yearThe graduates of O Level and A Level, in caps, gowns and maroon sashes.
  • Orientation DayAnnounced each yearA day that introduces students and families to the school year.
  • Book FairAnnounced each yearA fair of books, beside the library lessons of every class.
  • Education FairAnnounced each yearBeside the school's guidance on studying further.
  • Business WeekAnnounced each yearFor the business subjects and the Business Club.
  • Study TourAnnounced each yearField trips and outings, with parents' permission.

Culture4

  • Pohela Boishakh14 AprilThe Bengali new year, celebrated across the school, with an alpona in the courtyard.
  • Annual Cultural ProgrammeAnnounced each yearThe school's largest performance event: music, dance and drama on stage.
  • Annual DramaAnnounced each yearDrama on the hall stage, from the Drama activity and the Drama Club.
  • Annual MiladAnnounced each yearA gathering for prayer and recitation, held each year.

National days3

  • Shaheed Dibosh21 FebruaryThe Language Movement of 1952 remembered, and International Mother Language Day.
  • Independence Day26 MarchThe national day of Bangladesh, marked with a school function.
  • Victory Day16 DecemberThe victory of 1971, commemorated every year.

Sports1

  • Annual Sports DayAnnounced each yearThe march past, the displays and the races, on the school field.

Only the national days fall on a fixed date. The school announces the date of every other event each year. The school's own activities page also names community service and student workshops; they have no page yet.
