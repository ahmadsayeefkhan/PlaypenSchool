Playpen SchoolEstablished 1977

An English-medium school in Bashundhara, Dhaka, offering the Cambridge curriculum from Playgroup through to A Level. A balanced programme in a creative learning environment — the idea Playpen was founded on.

Cambridge curriculum from the early years through Ordinary, Advanced Subsidiary and Advanced Level.
