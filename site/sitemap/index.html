Learning

One path from Playgroup to A Level, in four stages.

See the 9 pages

  • JuniorClasses I to III. English, Bengali and Mathematics, with library lessons for every level.
  • MiddleClasses IV to VII. Core subjects learned by doing, and a new uniform from Class IV.
  • SeniorClasses VIII to XII. Electives in Class VIII, Cambridge O Level, then AS and A Level.
  • A LevelAS and A Level, taught at Playpen since 2013.
  • Cambridge pathwayO Level, AS and A Level explained in five minutes: what is sat when, how it is graded, what the words mean.

  • Student supportThe counsellor, career and university guidance and the health centre, for every class.

Events: the school year in one page

  • Annual Sports DayThe march past, the displays and the races, on the school field.
  • Pohela BoishakhThe Bengali new year, celebrated across the school, on 14 April.
  • Science and Technology FairStudent projects, experiments and models, shown at stalls in the hall.
  • Annual Cultural ProgrammeThe school's largest performance event: music, dance and drama on stage.
  • Graduation CeremonyThe graduates of O Level and A Level, in caps, gowns and maroon sashes.
  • Shaheed Dibosh21 February: the Language Movement remembered, and International Mother Language Day.
  • Independence Day26 March: the national day of Bangladesh, marked with a school function.
  • Victory Day16 December: the victory of 1971, commemorated every year.
  • Annual DramaDrama on the hall stage, from the Drama activity and the Drama Club.
  • Annual MiladA gathering for prayer and recitation, held each year.
  • Orientation DayA day that introduces students and families to the school year.
  • Book FairA fair of books, beside the library lessons of every class.
  • Education FairThe yearly fair beside the school's guidance on studying further.
  • Business WeekA week for the business subjects and the Business Club.
  • Study TourLearning away from the classroom, with parents' permission.
