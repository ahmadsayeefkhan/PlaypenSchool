Report summary · September 2026

The short version of the work.

Playpen’s existing site already held the school’s history, curriculum and campus. The redesign makes that information easier to find, lighter to load, and visible to search engines. Nothing below is a claim about traffic or enquiries. Those can only be measured after launch.

What changed

Forty-eight pages were reorganised into fourteen. Related facts now sit together. Every old address still lands on the right new page. The crest, the school’s own words, and the school’s own photographs were kept.

48 → 14 Pages Nothing was deleted. Every fact from the old site has a documented new home.
48 → 3 Files before the homepage shows The current homepage loads 24 stylesheets and 24 scripts. The new one uses three.
12.6 → 1.9 MB Homepage download Measured 16 September 2026, Lighthouse 13.4.1, mobile. Network requests: 93 → 19.
0 → 14 Pages Google can describe The old homepage had no search summary. Each new page has its own.

Also measured that day. Lighthouse mobile: performance 46 → 64, accessibility 82 → 98, best practices 77 → 100, SEO 92 → 100. The slowest visible part of the new homepage fell from 69.4 seconds to 5.6 seconds. Performance is still the weakest score, and the first fix after launch is the hero photograph.

Still waiting on the school

The preview is complete. It should not replace playpen.edu.bd until these are confirmed. All five were on the old site and could not be verified as still true.

  • Founding year. The old site says 1977 and 1997, and both “48 years” and “49”.
  • Subject lists. Twelve O Level and nine A Level subjects are published.
  • Phone numbers and emails. Eleven numbers and eight addresses across five departments.
  • Admissions. Which classes are open, and whether the two downloadable forms are current.
  • Named students. Twelve students are named with results. The child protection policy requires consent on record.
