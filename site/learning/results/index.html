Exams at home

Since 2020 the school has been a Cambridge exam venue, approved by the British Council. O Level and A Level students sit their exams in the school's own hall, in a building they know.

From the school's results summary for the May–June 2026 exam session. "Others" means all grades below C (below c at AS Level). What the grades mean.

02AchievementAwards and firsts

Honoured every year.

Every year, Playpen students receive The Daily Star Awards: for six or more A grades at O Level, and for three or more A grades at A Level. The record goes back to 2003, when the school's first O Level candidate earned straight A grades in all seven subjects. Many former students have since gone on to study at universities abroad.

  • 6+6 or more A grades at O Level for a Daily Star Award
  • 3+3 or more A grades at A Level for a Daily Star Award
  • 7/77 out of 7 subjects at grade A for the first O Level candidate, 2003

From the school's results summary for the May–June 2026 exam session and its history page.

Eleven competitions the school has published results for.

  1. 27 April 2024

    1st Space Exploration Olympiad

    Organised by the Bangladesh Innovation Forum at AIUB. Two students were 2nd runners-up.

    13th Bangla Olympiad

    A Class VII student took 1st position in Essay Writing. The group dance team also took first position.

  3. 24 February 2024

    Bakeman's 3rd International Language League

    Three Class IX students were runners-up for their presentation on Bangladeshi folk art.

  4. 23 February 2024

    16th National Abacus and Mental Arithmetic Competition

    One student was third runner-up.

  5. 9 February 2024

    Regional Physics Olympiad

    One student placed sixth in Category A.

  6. January 2024

    Scholastica Inter School Football

    Two individual awards, titled Best Player and Best Defender by the tournament.

    Math and Tech Fest

    Class VIII students took 1st position in Crypto Craft Chronicles. One student took first place in the Rube Goldberg competition and the campus ambassador award.

    Hurdco Inter School Football
