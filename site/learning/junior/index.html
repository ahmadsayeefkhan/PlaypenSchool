02What children learn hereThe Junior curriculum

A strong start in English, at a child's own pace.

The school says it builds the Junior curriculum so that children are never put under pressure. They learn at their own pace, and they build good behaviour, ways of speaking with other people and social manners as they go.

English comes first. The school treats it as the way in to every other subject (Mathematics, Science, Bengali and the rest), and says what children learn in these three classes prepares them for the next stage.

English
The foundation of the Junior curriculum, taught as the key to learning in every other subject.
Mathematics
Fundamental and logical ideas, beginning with addition and subtraction taught with visual aids.
Bengali
Reading, writing and recitation, which build a child's ability to communicate and an awareness of culture.

Built across these three classes

  • A strong foundation in English
  • Mathematics with visual aids
  • Reading and recitation in Bengali
  • Library lessons
  • Good behaviour
  • Social manners
03Library lessonsFor every level of the school

Reading has a lesson of its own.

Library classes run for every level of the school, and a Librarian looks after them. The school encourages children to read books, magazines, periodicals and newspapers from the library, to improve both their English and their ideas.

The library was modernised for the new campus, and the school bookshop is attached to it. See the campus.

Borrowing

If a book is not returned, the Librarian tells parents through the class teacher. If it still cannot be returned after a set period, the parent replaces it with a similar copy or pays its cost.

05How to joinThree steps for Classes I to III

Call, sit the test, hear.

  1. 01

    Call or visit.

    Call the admissions line: 01755 689 482 for Class I, or 01755 515 893 for Classes II and III, between 9.00 am and 1.00 pm. Or come and see the building.

  2. 02

    Sit the written test.

    The child sits a written test in English, Bengali and Mathematics.

  3. 03

    Interview and result.

    The Principal and the parents meet for an interview. The result comes by telephone.

Before you apply

The school accepts applications for the current year if there is space. Space changes by class and by year, so please call the admissions line before you apply.

06QuestionsFrom families in Classes I to III

Questions parents ask.

How is progress measured in Classes I to III?

The school year has two semesters, and a written examination ends each of them. The school uses a range of assessment tools alongside the formal papers and issues Progress Reports. To be considered for promotion, students must reach set passing marks in the core subjects. Examinations and reporting has the whole year.

How can I follow my child's progress during the year?

Through the school's parent and student portal, which shows academic performance, class tests, class work markings, homework, report cards, attendance and punctuality. Portalcurrent site (opens in a new tab)

What happens if my child loses a library book?

The Librarian tells parents through the class teacher when a book has not been returned. If the book still cannot be returned after a set period, the parent replaces it with a similar copy or pays its cost.

Which number do I call?

For Class I, the admissions line for Playgroup to Class I is 01755 689 482. For Classes II and III it is 01755 515 893. Lines are open 9.00 am to 1.00 pm. The contact page has every number.
