Middle School: Class IV to VII

Classes IV to VII. Core subjects taught with technology and hands-on work, two semesters a year, and an examination at the end of each.

02What children learn hereThe Middle curriculum

Core subjects, learned by doing.

In Classes IV to VII children study their core subjects through their textbooks and through discussion in class, so that ideas come from both. They use technology to extend what they learn, and they carry it further through hands-on work.

The school says its teachers design the curriculum around the students, and encourage each child to find a way of learning that suits them and to think for themselves.

Built across these four classes

  • Core subjects
  • Technology in lessons
  • Hands-on work
  • Individual learning styles
  • Independent thinking
  • Bengali and cultural heritage
03Laboratories and uniformTwo things that change at this stage

Laboratories on the campus, a new uniform from Class IV.

The campus has laboratories for Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Computer Science. Classrooms, laboratories and the library are air-conditioned, and generators keep lessons going through a power cut.

Class IV is where the uniform changes. Girls wear salwar-kameez and boys wear full trousers. Uniforms are tailored to the school's specification, and sportswear is bought from the school. Starting at Playpen has the details.

05How to joinThree steps for Classes IV to VII

Call, sit the test, hear.

  1. 01

    Call or visit.

    Call the admissions line: 01755 515 893 for Classes IV to VI, or 01755 693 623 for Class VII, between 9.00 am and 1.00 pm. Or come and see the building.

  2. 02

    Sit the written test.

    The child sits a written test in English, Bengali and Mathematics.

  3. 03

    Interview and result.

    The Principal and the parents meet for an interview. The result comes by telephone.

Before you apply

The school accepts applications for the current year if there is space. Space changes by class and by year, so please call the admissions line before you apply.

  • The child's birth certificate

06QuestionsFrom families in Classes IV to VII

Questions parents ask.

How is the school year organised?

The year has two semesters, and a written examination ends each of them. Class tests, homework, report cards, attendance and punctuality are on the school's portal. The school states its passing-marks rule for promotion for its Junior and Senior sections. It has not said how that applies to Classes IV to VII, so please ask on the line for your child's class (see the last question below). Examinations and reporting explains the year in full.

When do children choose their O Level subjects?

In Class VIII, after the second-semester examinations. That is the first year of the Senior stage. Senior School lists the subjects and the minimum number.

May my child bring a mobile phone to school?

No. The school's code of conduct does not allow mobile phones for any student up to Class XII. School policies has the rest of the code.

Which number do I call?

For Classes IV to VI, the admissions line is 01755 515 893. For Class VII it is 01755 693 623. Lines are open 9.00 am to 1.00 pm. The contact page has every number.
