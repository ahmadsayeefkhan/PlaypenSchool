02The subjectsAs the school lists them

Nine subjects, and at least three for each student.

A Level instruction began at Playpen in 2013. A student takes at least three of the nine subjects below.

The school confirms the subjects it offers each academic year, depending on staffing and the number of students. If one matters to your plans, call 01755 693 623, between 9.00 am and 1.00 pm, before you apply.

03How to joinAdmission to Class XI

Call, collect the form, hear.

  1. 01

    Call about the intake.

    Call the admissions line for Classes VII to XII, 01755 693 623, between 9.00 am and 1.00 pm, to ask whether there is space.

  2. 02

    Collect the A Level form.

    The Front Office has it, and so does the school's website. A Level has its own form, separate from the one for Playgroup to Class IX. Download the AS and A Level form (PDF)current site (opens in a new tab)

  3. 03

    Hear the decision.

    Admission is based on the school's mock examinations and the O Level result. The Principal's decision comes by telephone.

04Looking aheadGuidance and what is not yet published

Guidance for university, at home and abroad.

The Senior Vice-Principal and the Teacher-in-Charge give guidance on career choices, university applications, procedures and the guidelines students need for higher study at home and abroad. Senior students receive recommendation letters, transcripts and testimonials for their applications, with weight on academic results and on activities outside the classroom.

Student support describes the counsellor and the other services.

Where students go next

The school has not published where its A Level students go on to study, so this page makes no claim about it. A list would need the school's evidence and each student's consent.

06QuestionsFrom families joining AS and A Level

Questions parents and students ask.

How many subjects must a student take?

At least three, from the nine the school lists: English Language, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Economics, Accounting, Business and Computer Science.

What is admission to Class XI based on?

On the school's mock examinations and the O Level result. The A Level form is separate from the one for Playgroup to Class IX, and the school decides by telephone.

Can a student who took O Level at another school apply?

The school's published rule is that Class XI admission is based on its mock examinations and the O Level result. It does not publish a separate rule for students from other schools, so please call 01755 693 623, between 9.00 am and 1.00 pm.

Is admission open now?

The school's current notice says A Level admission for July 2026 to June 2027 is open. Space can change, so call 01755 693 623, between 9.00 am and 1.00 pm, to check before you apply.
