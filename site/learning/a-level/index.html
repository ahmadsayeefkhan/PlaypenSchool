Eleven subjects, at least three for each student.

A Level instruction began at Playpen in 2013. A student takes at least three of the eleven subjects listed here; each is taught at AS and at A Level.

03Two yearsMocks, the O Level result, your own form

Admission by results, a form of its own.

  • The basis

    Mock examinations and the O Level result.

    Admission to Class XI is based on the school's own mock examinations and the student's O Level result. The Principal's decision comes by telephone.

  • The form

    A Level has its own form.

    It is separate from the form for Playgroup to Class IX. The Front Office has it, and so does the school's website. Download the AS and A Level form (PDF)current site (opens in a new tab)

  • The notice, as read in October 2026

    The school's notice offers A Level admission for July 2026 to June 2027.

    That is what the notice said when this page was written, in October 2026. Space can change, so call before you apply. The current notices are on the notices page.

07QuestionsFrom families joining AS and A Level

Questions students and parents ask.

How many subjects must a student take?

At least three, from the eleven the school lists: English Language, Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Computer Science, Economics, Accounting, Business and Psychology.

What is admission to Class XI based on?

On the school's mock examinations and the O Level result. The A Level form is separate from the one for Playgroup to Class IX, and the school decides by telephone.

Can a student who took O Level at another school apply?

The school's rule is the mock examinations and the O Level result. If your child sat O Level elsewhere, call the line for Classes VII to XII, listed above, before you apply.

Is admission open now?

The school's notice, as read in October 2026, offers A Level admission for July 2026 to June 2027. Space can change, so call the line for Classes VII to XII to check before you apply, or read the current notices.
