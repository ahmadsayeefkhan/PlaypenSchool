Elementary School: Playgroup to KG II

Playgroup, Nursery, KG I and KG II: the first classrooms. Admission is by assessment, and there is no uniform until KG I.

02What children learn hereThe Elementary curriculum

The first classrooms.

The Elementary curriculum aims to develop a child's moral, mental and physical capabilities. It concentrates on the basic learning tools that build competence.

Teachers stay with the children through the day, so that social skills grow alongside academic ones.

Playgroup and Nursery
The focus is on communication and social skills, with attention to cognitive growth and the practice of logical thinking. Fine motor skills are built through structured activity.
Playgroup
Children develop self-esteem and the foundational skills that shape their later attitude to study.
KG I and KG II
Emotional, physical and mental growth, supported by trained teachers and activity-based learning, with safety and confidence first.

Built across the first years

  • Functional English
  • Fine motor skills
  • Group work
  • Discipline and punctuality
  • Social skills
  • Activity-based learning

04How to joinThree steps for Playgroup to KG II

Call, meet, hear.

  1. 01

    Call or visit.

    Call the admissions line for Playgroup to Class I, 01755 689 482, between 9.00 am and 1.00 pm, or come and see the building.

  2. 02

    Assessment or test.

    Playgroup and Nursery children sit an assessment. From KG I, the child sits a written test in English, Bengali and Mathematics.

  3. 03

    Interview and result.

    The Principal and the parents meet for an interview. The result comes by telephone.

Uniform

There is no uniform for Playgroup and Nursery. A uniform is compulsory from KG I. The details are on Starting at Playpen.

05QuestionsFrom families joining Playgroup to KG II

Questions parents ask.

How is my child assessed for admission?

Children applying for Playgroup and Nursery sit an assessment. From KG I the child sits a written test in English, Bengali and Mathematics. The Principal and the parents then meet for an interview. The full list of steps is on How to apply.

Does my child need a uniform?

Not in Playgroup or Nursery: children wear casual clothes. The uniform is compulsory from KG I. Starting at Playpen has the details.

Which number do I call?

The admissions line for Playgroup to Class I is 01755 689 482. Lines are open 9.00 am to 1.00 pm. For anything else, the numbers for each section are on the contact page.
