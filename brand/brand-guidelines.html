Playpen School
Brand Guidelines

Everything needed to produce on-brand work for Playpen — specific enough that a designer who has never worked with the school could get it right the first time.

Version 1.0 · 2026 · Prepared alongside the website redesign

01Brand foundation

A visual identity without a foundation is decoration. Everything in this document traces back to the five statements below.

Positioning

For families in Dhaka choosing an English-medium education, Playpen is the Cambridge school that carries a child from Playgroup to A Level in one continuous, unhurried programme — on a campus built for the purpose, by teachers who stay.

Each clause is doing work. One continuous programme is the structural advantage over schools a family must leave at Class V. Unhurried is the school’s own language about pressure. Built for the purpose distinguishes the 2018 campus from converted buildings. Teachers who stay is supported by faculty with thirty and forty years of service.

Mission

To give every student a well-balanced programme in a creative learning environment — grounding them in history and culture, giving them real proficiency in language, and providing a practical education they can apply to daily life.

Vision

That every child who passes through Playpen reaches their full potential, and leaves prepared to take part in the world as a global citizen of the 21st century.

Values

ValueWhat it means in practice
CareTwo teachers in every junior classroom. A counsellor on standby. The sentence “we assure you of the best possible care and education for your child” treated as a commitment, not a slogan.
BalanceAcademics and everything beside them. Believing that academic excellence alone does not guarantee a sound education.
Curiosity“Children must be taught how to think, not what to think.” Students encouraged to think outside the box, from Junior School upward.
RootednessEnglish as the medium, with deliberate emphasis on Bengali and on Bangladesh’s cultural heritage and secular community values.
ContinuityOne curriculum, one campus, one community from three years old to eighteen. Forty-eight years of it.

Personality

Playpen behaves like a settled, attentive, unshowy institution. It is the teacher who remembers your child’s name and does not need to tell you they are the best in the city. Four adjectives govern every piece of communication: warm, specific, plain, settled.

Clear space

Clear space on all four sides equals the height of the shield’s crown — the vertical distance from the top edge of the shield to the centre of the middle star. In the SVG that is 26 units on a 120-unit shield, or roughly 22% of the mark’s height. Nothing enters that zone: no text, no rules, no page furniture, no photographic detail.

Prohibited

  • Stretching, squashing, skewing or rotating any mark.
  • Adding drop shadows, glows, bevels, outlines or strokes.
  • Recolouring the shield field to anything other than Crimson, Deep Maroon, or one flat ink.
  • Removing, reordering or changing the count of the five stars.
  • Rebuilding the wordmark in a different typeface, or re-tracking it.
  • Placing the crest on a busy photograph without a colour block behind it.
  • Using the crest below 100px — substitute the shield mark instead.
  • Enclosing any mark in a box, circle or badge it did not ship with.

Co-branding

When Playpen appears beside a partner mark — Cambridge Assessment International Education, the British Council, a competition organiser — the partner mark sits to the right, separated by a 1px Bronze vertical rule with clear space on both sides. Neither mark exceeds the other’s cap height by more than 15%. Playpen always leads on Playpen-owned surfaces.

Production note on the SVG files

The supplied SVGs set the wordmark as live text in Fraunces and Plus Jakarta Sans. That is correct for web, where the fonts load. For print, outline the text before sending to a printer so the letterforms cannot substitute. Ask the supplier for outlined EPS and PDF versions to keep on file.

Application rules

  • Gold is a hairline, not a fill. Rules, borders, small figures, icons on dark. A large gold field makes the crest look like costume jewellery.
  • Gold text becomes Bronze on light ground. No exceptions — Playpen Gold fails AA on Parchment at 2.4:1.
  • One accent per element. Crimson carries the action; Bronze or Gold carries the label. Never both on the same component.
  • Sections alternate Parchment → Sand → Deep Maroon. Never two dark sections back to back.
  • No colour outside this list. That includes the brighter reds and the blue-greys that drifted into the old site.
  • No gradients except the gold border inside the shield mark, and the Deep Maroon scrims over photography.

Print production

Always supply CMYK for print, never RGB or hex. Pantone references above are nearest match and must be proofed against a physical swatch before any bulk run — particularly the gold, which shifts badly between coated and uncoated stock. Bleed 3mm all sides; keep critical content 5mm inside the trim.

Rules

  • Fraunces sets headings only. It is never used for body copy — it tires the eye past two lines.
  • Body copy never below 16px on screen, or 10pt in print.
  • Measure capped at 68 characters. Beyond that the eye loses the line return.
  • Headings take negative tracking; body copy takes none.
  • Italic Fraunces marks exactly one emphasised phrase per page, in the hero. Never more.
  • Two families is a hard limit — in decks, PDFs and merchandise as well as on the web.
  • Never set Fraunces in all caps. Uppercase belongs to Plus Jakarta Sans 600 with 0.18em tracking.
  • Use text-wrap: balance on headings so short lines do not orphan a single word.

05Imagery

Photography is where this brand is either credible or not. Parents in Dhaka can spot a stock library image instantly, and it costs the school trust every time.

Photography direction

DimensionStandard
SubjectReal Playpen students and staff, mid-activity. Writing, climbing, performing, experimenting, competing, helping. Never a line-up facing the lens.
PeopleTeachers in frame with students wherever possible — it is the visual proof of “individual attention”. Show the full age range across a page set.
LightNatural daylight. The campus has good light on the playground, in the gallery spaces and on the field. Avoid direct flash and mixed fluorescent colour casts.
CompositionAlternate scale: a wide frame that shows the building or the field, then a close frame that shows one face. Leave headroom on frames that will carry text.
ColourNatural. Do not tint or duotone. If an image clashes badly with the palette, replace it rather than filter it.
EventsPhotograph the people in front of the banner, not the banner. A stage backdrop is not a subject.

Treatment

  • Corner radius 4px on every image container.
  • On dark sections, apply a Deep Maroon gradient at 60–95% opacity from the bottom so type stays legible.
  • On light sections, images are untinted.
  • Aspect ratios: 4 : 3 cards, 3 : 4 portraits, 16 : 10 full-width, 1 : 1 gallery tiles.
  • Ship at a maximum of 1800px on the long edge, JPEG quality 78–82. Nothing on the site should exceed 400KB.
  • Every image needs alt text describing what is happening, not what the file is called.

Consent

Obtain and record parent or student consent before publishing an identifiable student, by name or by face. Keep the consent register with the school office and check it before each publication. Where a family withdraws consent, remove the image from the site, the gallery and any cached social post.

Illustration & iconography

Playpen does not use illustration. Where a diagram is genuinely needed — the admission process, the stage structure — build it from type, rules and numbers in the brand palette rather than drawing characters.

Icons are stroke-based at 1.4–1.6px on a 20px grid, rounded caps and joins, drawn in Bronze on light and Gold on dark. Icons never carry meaning on their own; they always sit beside a label.

06Voice & tone

Playpen has enough real evidence that it never needs a superlative. State the specific thing and let the reader draw the conclusion.

Voice attributes

AttributeWhat it meansWhat it is not
WarmWrites to a parent, not a market segment. Assumes the reader is anxious and treats that seriously.Sentimental. Cute about children. Gushing.
Specific“Junior classes have both a Class Teacher and an Assistant Teacher.” Numbers, names, dates, subjects.“A world-class holistic educational ecosystem.”
PlainShort sentences. Active voice. One idea per paragraph. The word a parent would use.Bureaucratic. A notice written as a decree.
SettledThe age is stated once and then demonstrated. The school has nothing to prove.Nostalgic. Defensive about competitors.

Vocabulary

Use consistently

students · parents · teachers · school · Cambridge curriculum · CAIE · O Level · A Level · learning · wellbeing · student support · Playgroup · Class I–XII

Avoid

best · No. 1 · world-class · unparalleled · premier · leverage · state-of-the-art (unless quoting the school’s own lab copy) · guaranteed admission · CIE (use CAIE) · switching between pupils / children / learners / students

House style

  • Class numbering: Roman numerals — Class I, Class VIII, Class XII. Never “Class 8”.
  • Stages: Elementary School, Junior School, Middle School, Senior School. Capitalised, always with “School”.
  • Qualifications: O Level and A Level — no hyphen, no apostrophe, both words capitalised. “AS Level” for Advanced Subsidiary.
  • Examination board: Cambridge Assessment International Education on first mention, CAIE thereafter. Never CIE.
  • Academic year: July 2026 – June 2027, with an en dash and spaces.
  • Dates: 19 July 2018. Day, month, year — no ordinal suffix.
  • Phone numbers: grouped as 01755 515 885 for display, full international format in tel: links.
  • Spelling: British English throughout — programme, organised, enrolment, practise (verb).
  • Time-sensitive pages carry a visible Last updated: Month Year.

Motion

  • Scroll reveals: 26px rise plus fade, 0.9s, --ease, triggered at 8% into the viewport, once only.
  • Stagger between siblings: 0.09s, up to five steps.
  • Hover transitions: 0.3s. Page-level transitions: 0.6–1.2s.
  • Fast in, slow out. Everything decelerates into its final position.
  • prefers-reduced-motion: reduce disables every animation and shows all content immediately. This is not optional.

  • Profile image: the shield mark on Maroon Ink with a 2px Gold ring, at 400 × 400px.
  • Cover image: a real campus photograph with a Deep Maroon gradient from the left and the inverted lockup on that side.
  • Posts: a photograph carries the post. Where a graphic is needed, Deep Maroon ground, Fraunces headline, Gold overline, shield mark bottom right at 48px.

Title slide: Maroon Ink ground, Fraunces headline in white, Gold overline, inverted lockup bottom left. Content slides: Parchment ground, Crimson H2, Ink body at 18pt minimum. Email

Inline styles only, single column at 600px, web-safe fallbacks declared on every rule. The logo goes in as a linked PNG at 2× resolution with alt text. Crimson buttons are built as bulletproof table cells, not background images.

Signage & environment

Deep Maroon ground with the heritage crest at scale, a 1pt gold rule, and the descriptor tracked to 0.2em. Nothing else on the surface. Wayfinding uses Plus Jakarta Sans 600 in white on Deep Maroon, with arrow glyphs matching the site’s icon weight.

09Accessibility

Most of Playpen’s audience reads this on a phone, often on a mid-range Android device, sometimes in bright daylight. Accessibility here is not compliance theatre — it is whether a parent can read the admission deadline.

  • All text meets WCAG AA. Body copy and headings on Parchment exceed AAA.
  • Body type never below 16px. Captions never below 12px, and never carrying essential information alone.
  • Every interactive element keeps a visible focus ring — 2px Crimson at 3px offset. Never outline: none.
  • Tap targets at least 44 × 44px.
  • Every image has alt text. Decorative images take alt="".
  • Heading levels descend in order. One h1 per page.
  • A skip link to main content is the first focusable element on every page.
  • Colour never carries meaning on its own — a notice tag has a word as well as a colour.
  • prefers-reduced-motion is fully honoured, including the hero crossfade and all scroll reveals.
  • Forms have visible labels, not placeholder-only fields, and errors are announced in text.

Brand approval

Any new asset carrying the crest — uniform, merchandise, signage, a partner’s material, a vehicle wrap — is approved by the Principal’s office against the audit checklist below before production. Supply the vendor with this document and the logo folder, not a screenshot of the website.

11Audit checklist

Run this over any asset before it is printed, posted or published.

  • Colours are exact palette values — no approximations, no off-brand reds.
  • Gold appears as a hairline or small accent, never as a large fill.
  • Gold text on light ground has been changed to Bronze.
  • Typefaces are Fraunces and Plus Jakarta Sans only, in the correct weights.
  • Body text is at least 16px on screen, 10pt in print, and under 68 characters per line.
  • The correct logo variant is used for the size and background, with full clear space.
  • The logo is not stretched, rotated, shadowed, outlined or recoloured.
  • Photography is genuine Playpen material, unposed, with consent on record.
  • Every image has meaningful alt text or is marked decorative.
  • Copy matches the voice attributes — no superlatives, no unsupported claims.
  • House style is applied: Roman class numerals, CAIE not CIE, British spelling.
  • Dates, phone numbers, email addresses and subject lists have been verified as current.
  • Contrast passes AA for every text and UI element.
  • Any co-branding follows the partner sizing and spacing rules.
  • Time-sensitive content carries a Last updated date.
